DHAN NOW Customer Consent, Authorisation and Acknowledgement

1. Purpose of Customer Consent

DHAN NOW is committed to providing customers with clear information about the digital loan application process, personal data processing, identity verification, credit assessment, loan servicing, repayment arrangements and applicable terms.

Before submitting a loan application or accepting a loan offer, customers must be provided with the relevant disclosures and asked to give their consent for the specific activities that require it.

Consent must be informed, specific, clear, affirmative and recorded through an appropriate digital or written process. Customers must not be treated as having consented merely because they visited the website, downloaded the application or continued browsing.

2. Consent for Loan Application

By selecting the relevant consent checkbox and submitting a loan application, the customer confirms that they:

  • Have read and understood the information displayed during the application process.
  • Are voluntarily applying for a loan.
  • Have provided information that is accurate and complete to the best of their knowledge.
  • Authorise the relevant entity or entities identified in the application to process the information necessary to evaluate the loan application.
  • Understand that submitting an application does not guarantee loan approval or disbursement.
  • Understand that the final loan amount, tenure, applicable charges, interest rate, repayment obligations and other terms will be communicated before acceptance of the loan offer.

The customer must be shown the applicable privacy notice and relevant lender details before providing the corresponding consent.

3. Consent for Personal Data Collection and Processing

The customer may be asked to provide personal information necessary for the application, identity verification, credit assessment, fraud prevention, loan servicing and compliance with applicable legal requirements.

Depending on the product and the customer's application, such information may include:

  • Full name and date of birth.
  • Mobile number and email address.
  • Residential and communication address.
  • PAN and other permitted identity documents.
  • Employment, occupation and income information.
  • Bank account details required for verification and disbursement.
  • Loan application and repayment information.
  • Credit history and credit bureau information, where applicable.
  • Verification results and records of customer communications.
  • Technical information necessary for security, authentication and fraud prevention.

The applicable privacy notice must identify the categories of personal data collected, the purposes of processing, relevant recipients, retention arrangements and the methods available for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Personal data must not be collected or processed for unrelated purposes merely because the customer has applied for a loan.

4. Consent for KYC and Identity Verification

Where required for the application or by applicable law, the customer may be asked to complete identity verification and Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures.

These procedures may include:

  • PAN verification.
  • Verification of other permitted identity documents.
  • Address verification.
  • Photograph or selfie verification.
  • Face matching and liveness checks.
  • Video-based verification, where applicable.
  • DigiLocker, CKYC or other authorised verification methods, where legally permitted.
  • Additional verification checks required by the relevant lender.

The customer will be informed about the verification method and the information required for that method.

Aadhaar-related authentication, offline verification or document access must be undertaken only through legally permitted methods and with the required consent or authorisation. Customers must not be compelled to provide Aadhaar where an alternative is legally required to be available.

5. Consent for Credit Bureau Checks and Credit Assessment

Where applicable, the customer authorises the relevant lender and its properly authorised service providers to obtain and process credit information for purposes such as:

  • Assessing the customer's creditworthiness.
  • Evaluating loan eligibility.
  • Determining an appropriate loan amount and repayment schedule.
  • Preventing fraud and duplicate applications.
  • Monitoring and servicing an approved loan.
  • Meeting applicable regulatory and legal obligations.

The applicable consent must identify the relevant purpose and, where required, the credit information company or category of recipient.

A credit bureau enquiry or credit assessment does not guarantee approval. The final decision will depend on the lender's applicable eligibility, underwriting and risk assessment criteria.

Credit information must be accessed, used and shared only in accordance with applicable law and the permissions obtained.

6. Consent for Bank Account Verification

The customer may be asked to provide bank account details or complete an account verification process to confirm the ownership or validity of the account.

Depending on the approved verification method, this may include account validation, penny-drop verification or another permitted banking verification service.

The customer authorises the relevant entity and its authorised service provider to use the information strictly for the disclosed verification and loan-related purposes.

Access to bank statements or transaction information must require the appropriate separate authorisation where applicable. Account Aggregator-based data access must follow the applicable consent process and cannot be assumed merely from this general consent.

7. Consent for Loan Disbursement

If the loan is approved and accepted, the customer authorises the actual lender, as identified in the applicable Key Facts Statement (KFS), sanction communication and loan agreement, to disburse the approved loan amount through the permitted disbursement process.

Where the approved loan structure permits deductions from the sanctioned amount, the customer must be shown the applicable deductions and the resulting net disbursal amount before accepting the loan offer.

The customer acknowledges that the amount credited to the bank account may differ from the sanctioned principal where disclosed charges are deducted in accordance with the applicable loan documents.

The actual lender and the entity responsible for disbursement must be clearly identified in the relevant documents. The website must not imply that DHAN NOW is the lender unless that statement accurately reflects the legal lending arrangement.

8. Consent for Processing Fee, GST and Interest Disclosures

Before accepting the loan, the customer must be provided with the applicable pricing information, including:

  • Sanctioned loan principal.
  • Processing fee.
  • Applicable GST on the processing fee.
  • Interest and the method used to calculate it.
  • Tenure and due date.
  • Total deductions from the sanctioned amount, where applicable.
  • Net amount to be disbursed.
  • Total repayment obligation.
  • Applicable APR and other disclosures required by law.
  • Late-payment charges or other applicable charges, where permitted and disclosed.
  • Repayment, extension or EMI-conversion terms, where such options are offered.

For the standard DHAN NOW 14-day product, the displayed pricing structure is a processing fee of 10% of the principal, GST at 18% of the processing fee and interest calculated at 1% per day for 14 calendar days on the original principal.

For example, on a sanctioned principal of ₹1,000:

  • Processing fee: ₹100.
  • GST on processing fee: ₹18.
  • Interest for 14 days: ₹140.
  • Total upfront deductions: ₹258.
  • Net disbursal: ₹742.
  • Scheduled repayment on the 14th day: ₹1,000.

The customer must be shown the actual applicable figures for their specific loan offer. The example above is illustrative of the stated pricing structure and does not replace the customer's KFS or loan agreement.

The applicable APR must be calculated and disclosed separately in accordance with the prescribed methodology and actual loan cash flows. The 14-day interest rate must not be represented as the APR.

9. Consent for Repayment and eNACH Mandate

Where electronic repayment arrangements are offered, the customer may be asked to authorise repayment through an approved payment method or electronic mandate, including eNACH where applicable.

Before authorisation, the customer must be informed of the relevant mandate details, including:

  • The entity in whose favour the mandate is established.
  • The purpose of the mandate.
  • The permitted debit amount or applicable limit.
  • The frequency or validity period.
  • The relevant bank account or payment instrument.
  • The procedure for cancellation or modification, where available.
  • The consequences of a failed or delayed repayment.

An eNACH mandate must be created only after the customer completes the required authorisation process.

A general website consent checkbox must not be treated as a substitute for the separate mandate authorisation required by the relevant banking or payment system.

The actual mandate beneficiary and repayment recipient must be consistent with the approved lending arrangement and applicable loan documents.

10. Consent for Loan Servicing and Customer Communications

The customer may be contacted through the contact details provided during the application for legitimate loan-related purposes, including:

  • Application status updates.
  • Requests for missing information or documents.
  • KYC and verification communications.
  • Loan approval or rejection notifications.
  • Disbursement confirmation.
  • Repayment reminders.
  • Payment receipts and account statements.
  • Information about overdue payments.
  • Customer support and grievance resolution.
  • Mandatory legal or regulatory communications.

Communications may be sent by SMS, telephone, email, WhatsApp or other permitted channels, subject to applicable law and the customer's preferences where relevant.

Loan-related communications must be professional, accurate and respectful. Contact information must not be used for unlawful harassment, intimidation or unauthorised disclosure of the customer's loan information.

Marketing and promotional communications must be handled separately wherever separate consent or preference management is required.

11. Consent for Sharing Information with Authorised Service Providers

Where necessary for the disclosed purposes, relevant information may be shared with appropriately authorised service providers, such as:

  • KYC and identity verification providers.
  • Credit information companies, where permitted.
  • Banking and payment service providers.
  • Technology, hosting and cybersecurity providers.
  • Fraud detection and risk assessment providers.
  • Customer support and communication service providers.
  • Electronic signature and mandate service providers.
  • Auditors, professional advisers or other recipients where legally permitted or required.

Information must be shared only to the extent necessary for the relevant purpose and subject to applicable legal, contractual and security requirements.

The relevant privacy notice must explain the categories of recipients and the purposes for which information may be shared.

Personal information must not be sold or disclosed to unauthorised third parties for unrelated purposes.

12. Consent for Electronic Records and Digital Signatures

Where permitted by law, the customer may receive application documents, loan disclosures, the KFS, the sanction communication, the loan agreement, repayment schedules and other relevant records electronically.

Where an electronic signature or digital acceptance is required, the customer must complete the applicable authentication and acceptance process.

The customer should be able to access or obtain copies of the documents they accept, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

The relevant records must accurately identify the parties, document version, date and time of acceptance, and other evidence required to establish a valid transaction.

13. Consent for Loan Extension or EMI Conversion

Any extension, rollover, EMI conversion or other repayment restructuring option must be offered only through an approved process and where permitted by the applicable lending arrangement.

Before accepting an alternative repayment arrangement, the customer must be provided with the revised terms, including:

  • Revised repayment dates.
  • Additional interest or other applicable charges.
  • Applicable GST, if any.
  • Revised instalment amounts.
  • Total repayment obligation.
  • Any change in tenure or other material terms.

For example, where a 20-day EMI-conversion option is available under the applicable product structure, the interest is calculated at 1% per day on the original principal for 20 days, with the resulting total payable amount divided into two equal instalments.

For a ₹1,000 principal, the illustrative calculation is ₹200 interest, ₹1,200 total repayment and two instalments of ₹600 each.

This option is not automatic. The customer must review and accept the specific offer and its terms before the repayment arrangement is changed.

A general consent for the original loan does not constitute acceptance of a future extension, rollover or EMI-conversion offer.

14. Voluntary Consent and Withdrawal

Where consent is the applicable legal basis for processing, customers may withdraw that consent through the method specified in the relevant privacy notice or consent interface.

Withdrawal will not invalidate processing lawfully completed before withdrawal. It may, however, affect services that depend on the withdrawn consent.

Withdrawal of consent does not automatically cancel an existing loan agreement, remove a repayment obligation or eliminate records that must be retained under applicable law.

Where processing is required by law or is based on another lawful ground, withdrawal of consent may not stop that processing.

15. Customer Declaration

Before submitting an application or accepting a loan, the customer may be asked to confirm the following declaration through an affirmative action:

“I confirm that I have read and understood the information presented to me regarding my loan application, personal data processing, identity verification, credit assessment, applicable loan charges and repayment obligations. I understand that my application is subject to eligibility checks and approval by the relevant lender. I acknowledge that the final loan terms will be provided in the applicable Key Facts Statement and loan agreement. I understand that separate consent or authorisation may be required for specific activities, including certain verification methods, bank data access, electronic signatures and repayment mandates.”

This declaration must not replace any separate consent, disclosure or authorisation required for a specific activity.

16. Recommended Website Consent Checkboxes

The website or application should use clear, separate consent controls wherever appropriate. The following wording may be adapted to the actual application workflow.

A. Application and Privacy Notice

“I have read the Privacy Policy and understand how my personal information will be processed for the purposes described in the notice.”

B. KYC and Identity Verification

“I authorise the relevant entity to carry out the identity verification procedures described to me, using the selected verification method and subject to applicable law.”

C. Credit Bureau and Credit Assessment

“I authorise the relevant lender and its authorised service providers to obtain and process my credit information for the purposes disclosed in the application.”

D. Bank Account Verification

“I authorise the disclosed account verification process for the purpose explained to me.”

E. Electronic Loan Documents

“I agree to receive the relevant loan documents and disclosures electronically, where permitted by law.”

F. Repayment Mandate

“I have reviewed the mandate details and separately authorise the repayment mandate through the applicable authentication process.”

G. Loan Offer Acceptance

“I have reviewed the applicable Key Facts Statement, loan amount, charges, interest, APR, tenure, net disbursal and repayment schedule, and I accept the loan terms presented to me.”

H. Optional Marketing Communications

“I agree to receive promotional communications from DHAN NOW through the channels described in the applicable consent notice.”

The marketing checkbox must be optional and separate from consent required to process a loan application. Declining optional marketing consent must not, by itself, prevent a customer from applying for a loan.

The website must not use pre-ticked boxes, bundled optional consents or misleading button labels to obtain consent.

17. Consent Records and Audit Trail

The relevant entity must maintain appropriate records of consent and authorisation, including, where applicable:

  • Customer or application reference number.
  • Date and time of consent.
  • Consent wording and notice version presented to the customer.
  • Specific purpose or activity authorised.
  • Affirmative action taken by the customer.
  • Authentication or verification evidence, where applicable.
  • Subsequent changes or withdrawal of consent.
  • Relevant document or mandate reference.

Consent records must be protected against unauthorised access, alteration or deletion and retained in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

18. Actual Lender and Entity Identification

The customer must be clearly informed of the identity of the actual lender and the role of DHAN NOW and any other participating entity.

The lender identified in the applicable KFS, sanction communication and loan agreement must match the actual lending arrangement.

Where a separate technology platform, Lending Service Provider (LSP), servicing entity or mandate-processing entity participates in the process, its role must be accurately described. Consent must not be used to obscure the identity of the lender or to authorise an entity to perform activities it is not legally permitted to perform.

20. Important Implementation Requirement

The consent wording displayed to customers must correspond to the actual data flows, verification methods, service providers, lender arrangements and repayment processes used by DHAN NOW.

Separate consent must be obtained wherever required. A single general consent must not be treated as blanket authorisation for every form of personal data processing, credit bureau access, bank data access, electronic mandate, marketing communication or future change to loan terms.

The final implementation must be consistent with the applicable privacy notice, Key Facts Statement, loan agreement, repayment mandate and other relevant disclosures.
