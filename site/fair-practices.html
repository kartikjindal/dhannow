DHAN NOW Fair Treatment, Transparency and Responsible Digital Lending

Effective Date: [Insert Effective Date] Last Updated: [Insert Last Updated Date]

1. Introduction

DHAN NOW is committed to maintaining fairness, transparency, accountability and responsible conduct in its digital loan application and customer service processes.

This Fair Practices Code sets out the principles governing customer interactions, loan application processing, disclosure of loan terms, repayment communications, grievance handling and the treatment of customers throughout the loan lifecycle.

The Code is intended to promote clear communication, responsible customer service, appropriate data handling and compliance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

Where lending activities are undertaken by a regulated bank or Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), the actual lender remains responsible for complying with the laws and regulatory directions applicable to it. DHAN NOW and any participating service provider must perform their respective activities in accordance with their authorised roles and contractual arrangements.

2. Objectives of the Code

The objectives of this Code are to:

  • Treat customers fairly and respectfully.
  • Provide accurate and understandable information about loan products.
  • Disclose applicable fees, interest, repayment obligations and other material terms before the customer accepts a loan.
  • Avoid misleading statements, hidden charges and unfair practices.
  • Protect customer information and maintain appropriate confidentiality.
  • Support responsible loan application and credit assessment processes.
  • Ensure that customer complaints are handled through accessible grievance channels.
  • Promote fair and professional repayment communications.
  • Maintain appropriate records and accountability.
  • Support compliance with applicable RBI directions, consumer protection requirements and other relevant laws.

3. Fair Treatment of Customers

Customers will be treated with dignity, fairness and respect throughout the application, approval, disbursement, servicing and repayment processes.

DHAN NOW and its authorised representatives must not:

  • Discriminate unlawfully against customers.
  • Use abusive, threatening, humiliating or misleading language.
  • Misrepresent loan approval, eligibility or disbursement status.
  • Make false promises about guaranteed approval or guaranteed loan amounts.
  • Conceal material loan terms or applicable charges.
  • Use coercive or deceptive methods to obtain customer consent.
  • Disclose customer loan information to unauthorised persons.
  • Misrepresent the identity of the actual lender.
  • Engage in unlawful or unfair recovery practices.

All customer-facing information should be clear, accurate and consistent with the applicable loan documents.

4. Transparency in Loan Product Information

Before a customer submits or accepts a loan offer, the relevant information should be presented in a clear and understandable manner.

Depending on the product and applicable requirements, the information should include:

  • Name of the actual lender.
  • Loan amount sanctioned.
  • Loan tenure.
  • Interest rate and calculation method.
  • Processing fee and applicable GST.
  • Net loan amount to be disbursed.
  • Total repayment obligation.
  • Repayment due date or instalment schedule.
  • Annual Percentage Rate (APR), as applicable.
  • Late-payment charges and other applicable fees.
  • Conditions for extension or repayment restructuring.
  • Customer support and grievance redressal details.

The customer should receive the applicable Key Facts Statement (KFS), sanction communication and loan agreement before accepting the loan, in accordance with applicable requirements.

Information displayed on the website must not contradict the terms presented in the customer's actual loan documents.

5. Loan Application and Processing

Loan applications will be processed according to the applicable eligibility criteria, verification requirements and underwriting procedures.

The customer may be asked to provide identity, address, income, employment, bank account or other relevant information required for the application.

DHAN NOW and the relevant lender or authorised service providers will process the information only for disclosed and lawful purposes.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Approval and the final sanctioned amount are subject to the applicable lender's assessment and decision.

Where an application is rejected, any communication or explanation will be provided in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

6. Know Your Customer and Verification Procedures

Where required, customers must complete applicable Know Your Customer (KYC), identity verification and other checks before loan disbursement.

Verification may include permitted document checks, PAN verification, address verification, bank account verification, face matching, liveness checks or other authorised procedures.

Such procedures must be conducted in accordance with applicable law and the relevant consent requirements.

Customer information must be protected through appropriate access controls and security measures.

7. Loan Approval and Documentation

The customer must be informed of the loan terms before accepting the offer.

The applicable documents may include:

  • Loan sanction communication.
  • Key Facts Statement.
  • Loan agreement.
  • Repayment schedule.
  • Fee and charge disclosures.
  • Privacy notice and relevant consent records.
  • Electronic repayment mandate, where applicable.

The documents should identify the actual lender and clearly explain the customer's rights, obligations and repayment responsibilities.

Where electronic acceptance is used, the relevant authentication and recordkeeping requirements must be followed.

Customers should be provided with access to the documents they accept, subject to applicable law and the relevant process.

8. Disbursement of Loan Amount

Loan disbursement must be carried out by the actual lender or through a legally permitted arrangement consistent with applicable regulatory requirements.

The customer must be informed of the sanctioned principal, any permitted deductions and the net amount expected to be credited.

The disbursement process must follow applicable requirements concerning the destination account, identity verification and transaction records.

DHAN NOW must not describe itself as the lender unless it is legally the lender for the relevant product.

The actual lender and the role of each participating entity must be accurately disclosed in the applicable loan documents and customer communications.

9. Interest, Fees and Other Charges

All applicable fees, interest and other charges must be disclosed before the customer accepts the loan.

For the standard DHAN NOW 14-day product, the stated pricing structure is:

  • Processing Fee: 10% of the loan principal.
  • GST: 18% of the processing fee.
  • Interest: 1% per day for 14 calendar days, calculated on the original principal.
  • Scheduled Repayment: The original principal on the specified due date under the stated repayment structure.

The applicable APR must be calculated and disclosed separately using the prescribed methodology and actual cash flows. The 14-day interest rate must not be presented as the APR.

No undisclosed fee or charge should be imposed. Any change in applicable charges must be handled in accordance with the loan agreement, applicable law and required customer disclosures.

10. Repayment Terms and Customer Communication

Repayment dates, instalment amounts, permitted payment methods and any applicable charges must be communicated clearly.

Customers should be able to identify the loan or proposal using the reference information specified by DHAN NOW's approved payment process.

Available payment methods may include:

  • UPI.
  • Debit cards, including supported card networks.
  • Net banking.
  • Airtel Payments Bank, where available through the approved payment facility.

The actual payment methods available at the time of payment will be displayed on the relevant payment page.

Customers should be provided with confirmation or a receipt for successfully processed payments through the applicable system.

Any approved change to the repayment schedule must be communicated and documented appropriately.

11. Repayment Extension and EMI Conversion

Where an extension, rollover or EMI-conversion option is available, the customer must receive the applicable terms before accepting the change.

The disclosure should explain:

  • The revised tenure.
  • The interest calculation method.
  • Additional fees or taxes, where applicable.
  • Revised instalment amounts.
  • Revised due dates.
  • Total repayment obligation.
  • Any other material change to the original loan terms.

An extension or EMI conversion must not be treated as automatic merely because the customer has an existing loan.

The customer must complete the required acceptance process, and the relevant lender must approve the arrangement where approval is required.

12. Fair and Respectful Collection Practices

Repayment reminders and collection-related communications must be professional, factual and respectful.

Collection activities must comply with applicable law, regulatory directions and the actual lender's approved collection policy.

DHAN NOW and its authorised representatives must not:

  • Use threats, intimidation or abusive language.
  • Publicly shame or humiliate customers.
  • Contact unrelated persons to disclose confidential loan details without lawful authority.
  • Make misleading statements about legal proceedings or consequences.
  • Misrepresent themselves as government officials, police officers or court representatives.
  • Use unauthorised methods to obtain repayment.
  • Disclose customer information through public channels.
  • Engage in harassment or unlawful pressure.

Where a third-party collection agent is involved, the responsible lender must ensure that the applicable authorisation, conduct and oversight requirements are followed.

Customers should be informed of the appropriate support and grievance channels if they dispute a payment, balance or collection communication.

13. Privacy and Confidentiality

Customer information must be collected, accessed, used, stored and shared in accordance with the applicable privacy notice and legal requirements.

Access to customer information must be restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who require it for legitimate purposes.

Appropriate safeguards should be maintained to protect customer information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

Information must not be shared with unrelated third parties for undisclosed purposes.

Where a customer makes a privacy-related complaint or request, it should be handled through the applicable process.

14. Customer Consent

Consent must be obtained where required for personal data processing, identity verification, credit bureau access, electronic mandates, bank data access or other relevant activities.

Consent must be clear, informed, purpose-specific and recorded appropriately.

A general consent checkbox must not be used as a substitute for a separate authorisation required by law or by the relevant verification or payment system.

Optional marketing consent must be separated from consent necessary to process a loan application wherever required.

16. Responsible Lending and Customer Understanding

The relevant lender is responsible for applying its approved credit assessment and lending processes.

Customer-facing information should help customers understand the cost of borrowing, repayment obligations and consequences of failing to repay on time.

No communication should suggest that a customer is guaranteed approval, guaranteed an increased loan limit or assured of a particular credit outcome.

Loan amounts and subsequent increases remain subject to applicable eligibility checks and the lender's decision.

Customers should be encouraged to review the loan documents carefully and borrow only after understanding the repayment obligations.

17. Training, Monitoring and Internal Controls

Relevant personnel and authorised service providers should receive appropriate instructions on:

  • Fair customer treatment.
  • Accurate loan disclosures.
  • Customer consent and privacy.
  • Professional communication.
  • Complaint handling and escalation.
  • Payment and repayment procedures.
  • Responsible collection conduct.
  • Compliance with applicable laws and contractual obligations.

Complaints, operational errors and recurring customer concerns should be reviewed to identify improvements in service quality and internal controls.

The responsible entity should maintain appropriate records of relevant customer interactions, complaints and corrective actions.

18. Regulatory Compliance

This Code must be implemented consistently with the laws and regulatory directions applicable to the relevant activities and entities, including, where applicable:

  • Reserve Bank of India directions governing regulated lenders.
  • Applicable digital lending requirements.
  • Fair Practices Code requirements applicable to the actual lender.
  • Customer protection and grievance redressal requirements.
  • Applicable privacy and data protection laws.
  • Payment-system requirements.
  • Other applicable statutory obligations.

Where a regulatory requirement imposes a stricter obligation than this Code, the applicable legal or regulatory requirement will prevail.

19. Policy Review and Publication

This Fair Practices Code will be reviewed periodically and updated when required to reflect changes in business operations, applicable regulations or customer service procedures.

The current version will be made available on the DHAN NOW website.

21. Final Statement

DHAN NOW aims to maintain a fair, transparent and customer-focused digital lending experience. Customers are encouraged to review all applicable disclosures, understand the loan terms before acceptance and contact customer support or the Grievance Officer if they have questions or concerns.

Nothing in this Code limits any right available to a customer under applicable law.
