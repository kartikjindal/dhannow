Short-Term Personal Loan

1. Overview

DHAN NOW provides a digital platform for customers to access information about short-term personal loans and complete the applicable online application process.

Customers can review the available loan amount, tenure, processing fee, applicable taxes, interest, net disbursal and repayment obligations before accepting a loan offer.

Loan approval and disbursal are subject to the applicable eligibility criteria, verification, underwriting and lending terms.

3. Loan Amount Progression

The specified loan progression is:

₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 → ₹7,000 → ₹10,000

A customer may qualify for the next loan level after fully repaying the current loan on time or before the due date, subject to the applicable verification and underwriting requirements.

Loan Progression Table

Level 4₹4,000Full repayment on time or early
Level 5₹5,000Full repayment on time or early
Level 6₹7,000Full repayment on time or early
Level 7₹10,000Maximum specified loan level

Example: A customer receives ₹1,000 and repays the full scheduled amount before the due date. Following the required checks, the customer may qualify for ₹2,000 on the next application.

Late repayment: A loan repaid after its due date does not meet the on-time repayment condition for automatic progression. Any exception must follow the applicable authorised policy.

Partial repayment: Partial repayment does not satisfy the full-repayment requirement for progression.

A higher loan amount is not guaranteed. Each subsequent application remains subject to the applicable eligibility and underwriting assessment.

6. Interest Rate and Calculation

The specified interest rate is 1% per day, calculated as simple interest on the original loan principal for the specified 14-day tenure.

Formula:

Interest = Original Loan Principal × 1% × Number of Days

For a 14-day tenure:

Interest = Original Loan Principal × 14%

Interest is calculated on the original principal under this structure, not on a progressively compounded balance.

  • Processing fee.

  • Interest for 14 days.

Formula:

Total Upfront Deductions = Processing Fee + GST + 14-Day Interest

8. Net Disbursal Amount

Net disbursal is the amount the customer receives after the applicable upfront deductions.

Formula:

Net Disbursal = Loan Principal − Total Upfront Deductions

12. EMI Conversion Option

The specified 20-day EMI calculation uses simple interest of 1% per day on the original principal for 20 days, followed by two equal instalments.

Formula:

20-Day Interest = Original Principal × 1% × 20

Total Repayment = Original Principal + 20-Day Interest

Each EMI = Total Repayment ÷ 2

Examples

Loan PrincipalInterest for 20 DaysTotal RepaymentNumber of EMIsEach EMI

Example: Under this calculation, a ₹1,000 principal produces total repayment of ₹1,200 over 20 days, divided into two instalments of ₹600 each.

The EMI option must be presented as an available customer product only where it is permitted by the approved lending arrangement. The applicable repayment dates, APR, charges and instalment schedule must be disclosed before acceptance.

13. Special Repayment Arrangements

A special repayment or instalment arrangement may be considered in an eligible case only under the applicable authorised policy and approval process.

Such an arrangement must not be represented as an automatic customer entitlement.

Before implementation, the relevant parties must confirm:

  • Who has authority to approve the arrangement.
  • Whether the arrangement is permitted under the loan agreement and applicable rules.
  • The revised repayment dates and amounts.
  • Any applicable interest, fees or charges.
  • The customer's informed acceptance.
  • The required system records and customer communication.

The website must not promise an arrangement that is unavailable or not permitted under the applicable lending documents.

14. Late Repayment

Customers should repay the amount specified in their loan agreement by the due date.

If a customer anticipates difficulty making a repayment, they should contact customer support using the published contact details.

Any late-payment charge, penal charge or other consequence must be:

  • Permitted under the applicable agreement and law.
  • Disclosed to the customer as required.
  • Calculated according to the applicable policy.
  • Reflected accurately in the customer's account and communication.

No unapproved or undisclosed charge should be added to a customer's outstanding balance.

15. Prepayment and Early Repayment

Customers should refer to their loan agreement for the applicable rules on early repayment or prepayment.

The website must explain whether any charge applies, how the outstanding amount is calculated and how the customer can obtain confirmation that the loan has been closed.

Example: If a customer wants to repay before the scheduled due date, the customer should check the applicable agreement and obtain the correct repayment amount through the authorised customer channel.

Do not promise a refund of interest or fees unless the applicable terms and law provide for it.

16. Loan Agreement and KFS

Before accepting a loan, customers must be given access to the applicable Key Facts Statement (KFS) and loan agreement through the prescribed process.

The relevant disclosures should include, as applicable:

  • Actual lender's identity.
  • Loan principal.
  • Net amount disbursed.
  • Interest rate and APR.
  • Processing fee and applicable taxes.
  • Other applicable charges.
  • Repayment schedule and due dates.
  • Applicable late-payment or penal charges.
  • Customer-support and grievance details.
  • Other material terms required by the applicable rules.

The customer must be able to review the applicable terms before acceptance.

17. Important Customer Notice

All calculations in this page follow the specified DHAN NOW product structure. The customer-specific offer, applicable KFS and loan agreement determine the actual terms applicable to a particular loan.

The interest rate expressed as 1% per day is not itself the APR. APR must be calculated using the applicable cash flows and prescribed disclosure method, including relevant fees and charges.

Any extension, EMI conversion or special repayment arrangement must be implemented only where it is permitted by the applicable lending arrangement and customer documentation.
