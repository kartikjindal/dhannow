1. Introduction

This Payment, Refund & Transaction Dispute Policy explains the payment methods available through DHAN NOW, the procedures for making loan repayments, and the process for reporting failed, pending, duplicate, incorrect, or disputed transactions.

DHAN NOW aims to provide customers with a secure, transparent, and convenient payment experience. Customers should carefully review the payment details, loan or proposal reference, amount payable, and applicable terms before confirming a transaction.

This Policy must be read together with the applicable Key Fact Statement (KFS), loan agreement, repayment schedule, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and other applicable disclosures.

Where any provision of this Policy conflicts with applicable law or a binding regulatory requirement, the applicable law or regulatory requirement will prevail.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to customers who use an available payment facility to make a payment relating to a loan application, an approved loan, or an existing repayment obligation through DHAN NOW or its authorised payment channels.

It covers:

  • Available payment methods.
  • Online loan repayment procedures.
  • Failed, pending, and unsuccessful transactions.
  • Duplicate or excess payments.
  • Incorrect payment details.
  • Refund and reversal requests.
  • Disputes relating to payment status.
  • Payment receipts and transaction records.
  • Unauthorised or suspicious transactions.
  • Customer support and grievance escalation.

The treatment of a particular transaction will depend on its status, the applicable loan documentation, the payment method used, the relevant payment service provider, and applicable law.

3. Available Payment Methods

Subject to availability and display at checkout, customers may be able to make payments through the following methods:

  • Debit Cards: MasterCard, Visa, RuPay, and Maestro, where supported by the payment gateway.
  • UPI: Unified Payments Interface.
  • Net Banking: Participating banks supported by the payment gateway.
  • Airtel Payments Bank: Where enabled and available through the authorised payment facility.
  • Other Methods: Only where specifically displayed as available on the official payment page.

Important: The payment methods actually available at the time of a transaction will be those displayed on the official payment page. Availability may depend on the payment gateway, issuing bank, payment network, transaction limits, and service availability.

DHAN NOW does not guarantee that every payment method will be available for every transaction.

4. Identifying a Loan or Payment

Before initiating a payment, customers must correctly identify the loan or proposal to which the payment relates.

Customers may be asked to provide one of the following:

  • The mobile number linked to their loan account or loan application; or
  • Their loan proposal number or another reference specifically requested on the payment page.

Customers must verify the displayed information before proceeding.

An incorrect mobile number, loan reference, proposal number, or payment amount may delay transaction identification and reconciliation.

Customers should not make a payment against an unfamiliar loan reference or an amount they cannot independently verify.

5. Loan Repayment Procedure

Customers should follow the steps below when making an online repayment:

  • Visit the official DHAN NOW website or an authorised payment facility.
  • Select the applicable repayment option.
  • Enter the mobile number linked to the loan account or the required loan proposal number.
  • Verify the loan or proposal details displayed.
  • Review the amount payable and the relevant due date.
  • Select an available payment method.
  • Complete the payment using the bank or payment service provider's authorised process.
  • Wait for the transaction result.
  • Save the transaction reference, UPI reference, bank reference, payment receipt, or confirmation message, as applicable.

Customers should avoid initiating repeated payments merely because a confirmation message has not appeared. First check the transaction status with the bank or payment service provider and through the authorised customer support channel.

A payment is treated as received for loan-account purposes only after its status has been appropriately verified and reconciled with the relevant payment records.

6. Payment Confirmation and Receipts

Where supported by the payment system, a successful transaction may generate a confirmation screen, payment receipt, transaction reference, SMS, email, or other acknowledgement.

Customers should retain the available payment evidence until the transaction has been reflected correctly in the relevant loan account.

A bank debit alert or UPI success screen may not, by itself, establish that the payment has been correctly credited to the intended loan account. If the loan account is not updated within the expected processing period, the customer should contact support with the transaction details.

DHAN NOW or the responsible lender or payment service provider may verify the transaction using relevant records before confirming the payment status.

7. Failed Transactions

A transaction may fail because of:

  • Insufficient account balance.
  • Incorrect payment details.
  • Bank or payment gateway downtime.
  • Network connectivity issues.
  • Transaction limits imposed by the bank or payment network.
  • Authentication failure.
  • A timeout or interruption during payment.
  • Security controls or suspected fraudulent activity.
  • Other technical or operational reasons.

If the transaction fails and no amount is debited, the customer may retry after checking the details and confirming that the payment facility is operational.

If the amount is debited but the transaction is shown as failed, unsuccessful, or incomplete, the customer should:

  • Check the transaction status with the bank, card issuer, UPI application, or payment service provider.
  • Retain the transaction reference and debit evidence.
  • Avoid making another payment until the status has been reasonably checked, where practicable.
  • Contact DHAN NOW support if the payment is not reflected or the issue remains unresolved.

Any reversal or refund of a failed transaction will be handled in accordance with the applicable payment-network rules, payment service provider's procedures, bank processes, and applicable law.

8. Pending or Processing Transactions

A transaction may remain pending or processing even after the customer's bank account has been debited.

In such circumstances, the customer should not assume that the payment has either failed permanently or been successfully credited to the loan account.

The customer should retain the transaction reference and allow the relevant bank and payment service provider to complete the status check.

If the payment remains unresolved, the customer may contact DHAN NOW support and provide the required transaction evidence.

Where necessary, DHAN NOW may coordinate with the responsible payment service provider or lender to investigate the transaction.

The applicable repayment obligation, treatment of a payment made near its due date, and any consequences of a payment-processing delay will be determined in accordance with the loan agreement, applicable lender procedures, and applicable law. Customers should promptly report a delayed payment rather than assume that a pending transaction has satisfied the repayment obligation.

9. Duplicate Payments and Excess Payments

A duplicate payment may occur when a customer makes more than one payment towards the same obligation or when a transaction is repeated following a delay in confirmation.

If a customer believes that a duplicate or excess payment has been made, the customer should contact support and provide:

  • The registered mobile number or relevant loan reference.
  • The amount of each transaction.
  • The date and approximate time of payment.
  • The transaction IDs or bank reference numbers.
  • Available bank statements, payment receipts, or screenshots.
  • Any other information reasonably required to verify the transactions.

After verification, the responsible entity will determine whether the payment represents a duplicate, an excess amount, an outstanding liability, or another transaction category.

Any confirmed excess amount will be handled in accordance with the applicable loan agreement, repayment records, payment-network requirements, and applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, this may involve a refund or an appropriate adjustment to the loan account where legally and contractually permitted.

A refund or adjustment will not be processed solely on the basis of an unverified claim.

10. Incorrect Payments

Customers must carefully verify the loan reference and payment amount before confirming a transaction.

If a customer accidentally pays the wrong loan account, uses an incorrect reference, or transfers an amount to an unintended destination, the customer should contact support immediately.

The customer should provide the transaction reference, payment date, amount, relevant loan or proposal details, and supporting evidence.

DHAN NOW will review the information available and, where appropriate, coordinate with the responsible lender, bank, payment gateway, or other relevant entity.

Recovery of a payment made to an incorrect destination cannot be guaranteed. Any reversal, correction, or recovery will depend on transaction verification, the receiving entity's cooperation, applicable payment-system procedures, and applicable law.

Customers should not share their banking passwords, card PINs, UPI PINs, or OTPs with customer support or any person claiming to assist with payment recovery.

11. Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the nature of the transaction, the applicable loan documentation, the stage of the loan process, and applicable law.

A request for a refund will be reviewed on its individual merits and verified against the relevant records.

11.1 Eligible Refund or Reversal Requests

Subject to verification and applicable rules, a refund or reversal request may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • A confirmed duplicate payment.
  • A verified excess payment.
  • A successful debit for a transaction that was not completed or credited.
  • An erroneous charge established after investigation.
  • A refund required under the applicable loan agreement or KFS.
  • A refund required by applicable law or a binding regulatory direction.
  • Another transaction for which a refund is approved following verification.

The applicable bank, card issuer, UPI network, payment service provider, or responsible lender may need to participate in processing the refund.

11.2 Refunds of Loan Charges

Processing fees, applicable taxes, interest, or other loan-related charges will be treated according to the applicable KFS, loan agreement, and law.

A customer is not automatically entitled to a refund of every loan-related charge merely because the customer changes their mind, raises a general request, or repays the loan early.

Where a charge has been collected incorrectly, a refund is required by law, or the applicable loan terms provide for a refund, the matter will be reviewed and processed as required.

Any permitted cooling-off or look-up period, cancellation right, or refund entitlement applicable to a digital loan will be governed by the applicable regulatory requirements and the disclosures provided by the actual lender.

11.3 Loans Already Disbursed

Once a loan has been disbursed, a customer cannot assume that the loan can be cancelled merely by submitting a general refund request.

Any cancellation, exit, cooling-off, or repayment arrangement will be governed by the applicable loan agreement, KFS, actual lender's disclosed process, and applicable law.

Customers who wish to cancel or exit a loan should contact the actual lender through the officially disclosed channel and follow the applicable procedure.

A refund request does not, by itself, cancel an outstanding loan or suspend a repayment obligation.

12. Refund Processing and Timelines

Refund and reversal timelines may vary depending on:

  • The payment method used.
  • The issuing or receiving bank.
  • The card network or UPI payment system.
  • The payment gateway or payment service provider.
  • The transaction's current status.
  • The time required to verify the claim.
  • Any investigation or reconciliation required.
  • Applicable regulatory and payment-network timelines.

Once a refund is approved, it will be processed through the appropriate channel in accordance with the applicable process and prescribed timelines.

DHAN NOW will not represent an unverified refund as approved or guarantee a fixed credit date where the timing depends on a bank, payment network, or third-party payment provider.

If an approved refund has not been received within the communicated or applicable timeline, the customer should contact support with the refund reference and original transaction details.

13. Payment Disputes and Transaction Investigation

Customers may raise a dispute if:

  • A payment has been debited but not reflected in the loan account.
  • A payment has been credited for an incorrect amount.
  • A transaction appears more than once.
  • A refund has not been received.
  • A transaction is shown as successful but the customer disputes its status.
  • A transaction appears unauthorised.
  • A payment receipt or loan-account balance appears incorrect.
  • Another payment-related discrepancy requires investigation.

To help resolve a dispute, customers should provide:

  • Full name as recorded in the loan or application records.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Loan account number or proposal reference, where applicable.
  • Transaction date and amount.
  • Payment method used.
  • Transaction ID, UPI reference, bank reference, or refund reference.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Supporting evidence, such as a receipt or relevant transaction screenshot, with unnecessary sensitive information masked.

Customers should not email full card numbers, card security codes, UPI PINs, passwords, OTPs, or other authentication credentials.

The responsible team will review the available information and may request additional details that are reasonably necessary to investigate the dispute.

14. Unauthorised or Suspicious Transactions

Customers who identify an unfamiliar, suspicious, or potentially unauthorised payment should promptly contact their bank, card issuer, UPI application, or relevant payment service provider using its official contact channels.

Customers should also notify DHAN NOW support if the transaction appears to involve their DHAN NOW loan account or an authorised payment facility.

Where appropriate, the matter may be referred to the actual lender, payment service provider, or other relevant authority for investigation.

Customers should take immediate steps to secure compromised payment credentials through their bank or payment provider. DHAN NOW will never require a customer to disclose a UPI PIN, card PIN, password, or OTP to investigate a payment dispute.

The handling of unauthorised electronic transactions, including any applicable customer liability, reporting deadlines, and reimbursement requirements, will be governed by the relevant bank or payment-system rules and applicable law.

15. Repayment Obligations During a Dispute

Submitting a payment complaint or refund request does not automatically suspend a customer's loan repayment obligations.

If a customer has made a payment that is not reflected in the loan account, the customer should promptly report it and provide the transaction evidence so that the payment can be investigated.

The responsible lender will assess the transaction and determine the appropriate treatment of the repayment record in accordance with the loan agreement, applicable regulatory requirements, and verified transaction information.

No additional charge, adverse reporting, or other consequence will be imposed contrary to applicable law or binding regulatory requirements.

Customers should contact the actual lender promptly if a payment dispute may affect a repayment due date.

16. Payment Security and Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for taking reasonable precautions when making payments.

Customers should:

  • Use the official DHAN NOW website or an authorised payment link.
  • Check the payment amount and loan reference before confirming.
  • Keep payment receipts and transaction references.
  • Use secure devices and trusted internet connections where possible.
  • Never disclose OTPs, PINs, passwords, or card security codes.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious payment or refund links.
  • Report suspected fraud promptly to their bank and the relevant support channel.
  • Verify that any request for payment comes through an authorised channel.

DHAN NOW will use appropriate processes for handling payment-related information, subject to applicable law and the relevant privacy and security arrangements.

Customers should remain cautious of persons claiming that a refund can be released only after the customer shares an OTP or PIN or pays an unofficial processing charge.

17. Payment Records and Reconciliation

Payment information may be processed or reviewed for purposes including:

  • Confirming payment status.
  • Matching a payment to a loan account.
  • Reconciling bank and payment gateway records.
  • Investigating failed or disputed transactions.
  • Processing eligible refunds or reversals.
  • Maintaining accounting and audit records.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations.
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorised transactions.

Relevant transaction information may be shared with the actual lender, authorised payment service providers, banks, or other authorised parties where necessary for these purposes and permitted by applicable law.

Such processing and sharing will be subject to the applicable Privacy Policy and relevant legal requirements.

DHAN NOW may coordinate with the relevant provider to investigate a payment issue, but the final processing of a reversal or refund may depend on the responsible provider and applicable payment-system rules.

Nothing in this Policy limits any right or remedy available to a customer under applicable law.
