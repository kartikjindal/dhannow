About DHAN NOW

1. Who We Are

DHAN NOW is a digital platform associated with NAVIX Finance Private Limited, designed to provide customers with a convenient online experience for applying for short-term personal loans.

Our objective is to make the loan application process simple, transparent and accessible. We aim to help customers understand loan eligibility, applicable charges, repayment obligations and the steps involved in applying for a loan.

Through our digital platform, customers can access loan-related information, complete the required application process and review the applicable loan terms before accepting an offer.

6. Our Digital Loan Application Process

DHAN NOW is designed to provide customers with a structured digital application journey.

The process generally includes:

  • Online application.
  • Submission of the required information.
  • Identity and other applicable verification.
  • Eligibility and underwriting assessment.
  • Presentation of the applicable loan offer.
  • Review of the Key Facts Statement (KFS) and loan agreement.
  • Acceptance of the applicable terms and required consents.
  • Loan disbursal according to the approved lending arrangement.
  • Repayment through an available and authorised payment method.

The availability of a loan depends on the applicable eligibility criteria, verification results and lending decision. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.

9. Loan Amount Progression

The specified loan progression is:

₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 → ₹7,000 → ₹10,000

A customer may qualify for the next level after fully repaying the current loan on time or before the due date, subject to applicable verification and underwriting requirements.

Example: A customer takes a ₹1,000 loan and repays it in full before the due date. After the required checks, the customer may qualify for the next level of ₹2,000.

A loan upgrade is subject to the applicable policy and assessment; it is not guaranteed solely because the previous loan has been repaid.

12. Responsible Borrowing Commitment

Before accepting a loan, customers should:

  • Review the amount they will actually receive.
  • Understand the interest rate and APR.
  • Check processing fees, taxes and other applicable charges.
  • Confirm the total repayment obligation and due date.
  • Assess their ability to repay on time.
  • Read the KFS and loan agreement.
  • Use only authorised payment channels.
  • Contact customer support if any term is unclear.
