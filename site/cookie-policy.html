2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on a visitor's browser or device when the visitor accesses the website.

Cookies can help a website recognise a browser during a later visit, remember certain settings, maintain a session, or understand how visitors interact with website pages.

Other technologies, such as pixels, tags, local storage, and similar identifiers, may perform related functions. Where relevant, this Policy refers to these technologies collectively as “cookies and similar technologies.”

Cookies do not all perform the same function. Some are necessary for the website to operate, while others may be used for analytics, personalisation, or marketing, depending on the technologies implemented.

3. Why DHAN NOW May Use Cookies

Subject to the website configuration and applicable law, DHAN NOW may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • To enable essential website functionality.
  • To maintain secure browsing sessions.
  • To help protect against suspicious activity and unauthorised access.
  • To remember certain user preferences.
  • To understand website performance and visitor interactions.
  • To identify technical errors and improve website usability.
  • To measure the effectiveness of website content or campaigns, where applicable.
  • To support additional features where these have been implemented and appropriately disclosed.

The actual cookies used on the website will depend on the technologies and services deployed by DHAN NOW and its authorised service providers.

4. Types of Cookies

4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for essential website functions, such as maintaining a secure session, supporting navigation, protecting forms, or enabling requested services.

Without these cookies, certain website functions may not operate correctly.

Where permitted by applicable law, strictly necessary cookies may be used without optional-cookie consent when they are genuinely required to provide the requested service.

4.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may remember preferences or settings, such as selected language or other interface preferences, where these features are available.

They may help provide a more consistent experience when a visitor returns to the website.

4.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

If analytics tools are implemented, these cookies may help DHAN NOW understand how visitors use the website, which pages are visited, whether technical errors occur, and how website performance can be improved.

Where required by applicable law, these cookies will be activated only after the necessary consent has been obtained.

4.4 Advertising and Marketing Cookies

If advertising or marketing technologies are implemented, they may be used to measure campaigns, understand interactions with promotional content, or deliver relevant advertising.

Such technologies will be used only where implemented and subject to applicable consent and legal requirements.

DHAN NOW does not represent that advertising or marketing cookies are currently active unless the relevant technologies have actually been deployed.

5. Session and Persistent Cookies

Cookies may also be categorised according to how long they remain on a device.

Session Cookies: These are generally removed when the browser session ends. They may support functions that are required while a visitor is using the website.

Persistent Cookies: These remain on a device for a defined period or until they are deleted. They may help remember preferences or recognise a returning browser.

The duration of a cookie depends on its purpose, configuration, and the service that places it.

6. First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set directly by the website being visited.

Third-party cookies may be set by an external service integrated into the website, such as an analytics provider, embedded service, or other authorised technology provider.

Where third-party technologies are used, their collection and processing of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

DHAN NOW will use third-party technologies only as appropriate for the website's functions and subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Visitors should review the relevant third party's privacy information where they wish to understand its practices in greater detail.

10. How to Manage Cookies Through Your Browser

Most modern browsers provide settings that allow users to view, block, restrict, or delete cookies.

Visitors may be able to manage cookies through their browser settings, including by:

  • Opening the browser's Settings or Preferences menu.
  • Locating the Privacy, Security, or Cookies section.
  • Reviewing available cookie controls.
  • Deleting existing cookies where desired.
  • Blocking or restricting cookies according to the browser's available options.

The exact steps vary by browser, device, and software version.

Please note that blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect website functionality. For example, some sessions, preferences, or form features may not work as expected.

Browser-level controls may not automatically communicate or apply a visitor's preferences to every third-party technology.
