Your Digital Loan Journey with DHAN NOW

1. Overview

DHAN NOW provides a digital process through which eligible customers can apply for a short-term personal loan, complete the required verification, review the loan offer and understand their repayment obligations.

The process is designed to make each stage understandable, from submitting an application to repaying the loan.

Important: Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or disbursal. The final decision depends on the applicable lender's eligibility criteria, verification, underwriting and approval process.

2. Step-by-Step Loan Process

STEP 1 — Visit the DHAN NOW Website

Visit:

www.dhannow.com

Navigate to the loan application section and review the available product information, eligibility requirements, applicable charges and repayment terms.

Customer instruction: Read the available loan information before beginning your application.

STEP 2 — Enter Your Mobile Number

Enter the mobile number requested by the application and complete the required verification process.

The mobile number may be used for application-related communication, OTP verification and account identification, subject to the applicable privacy notice and consent requirements.

Example: A customer enters their mobile number and receives an OTP to verify access to that number.

Important: Customers should never share their OTP with an unauthorised person or anyone claiming to guarantee loan approval.

STEP 3 — Complete the Loan Application

Provide the information requested in the application form.

This may include:

  • Full legal name.
  • Date of birth.
  • Contact details.
  • Residential address.
  • Employment and income information, where required.
  • Bank account details.
  • Other information needed for eligibility and verification.

All information must be accurate and complete.

Example: A customer applies for a ₹1,000 loan and provides the required personal and financial details for assessment.

STEP 4 — Complete KYC Verification

Complete the applicable Know Your Customer (KYC) process.

Depending on the approved process, verification may include:

  • PAN verification.
  • Aadhaar-based verification where legally permitted and appropriately consented to.
  • Identity and address verification.
  • Bank account verification.
  • Face match or liveness checks, where applicable.
  • Other legally permitted verification checks.

The exact documents and checks depend on the applicable lender's process and regulatory requirements.

Customer instruction: Submit only genuine documents and ensure that the information provided matches the relevant records.

STEP 5 — Complete Financial and Eligibility Checks

The application may be assessed using the information provided by the customer and other permitted verification sources.

Checks may include:

  • Identity verification.
  • Income and repayment-capacity assessment.
  • Bank account verification.
  • Credit information checks, where permitted and appropriately authorised.
  • Fraud-prevention checks.
  • Existing obligations and other relevant underwriting factors.

The actual lender makes the lending decision according to the applicable lending arrangement.

Important: Passing one verification check does not guarantee loan approval.

STEP 6 — Receive the Loan Decision

After the required checks, the customer may receive an approval, rejection or request for additional information.

If approved, the customer will be shown the applicable loan offer.

The offer should clearly state:

  • Loan principal.
  • Processing fee.
  • GST on the processing fee, where applicable.
  • Interest rate.
  • APR.
  • Net disbursal amount.
  • Repayment amount.
  • Repayment due date.
  • Other applicable charges and conditions.

Example: A customer applies for ₹1,000. If the application is approved under the specified product structure, the customer can review the calculated deductions and net disbursal before deciding whether to accept the offer.

STEP 7 — Review the Loan Offer

Before accepting the loan, carefully review the financial details.

Under the specified 14-day calculation structure:

These figures illustrate the specified product calculation. The actual customer offer, KFS and loan agreement govern the transaction.

STEP 8 — Read the KFS and Loan Agreement

Before accepting the offer, the customer must be given access to the applicable Key Facts Statement (KFS) and loan agreement.

The customer should review:

  • The identity of the actual lender.
  • The loan amount and net disbursal.
  • The interest rate and APR.
  • Fees, taxes and other applicable charges.
  • Repayment amount and due date.
  • Applicable late-payment or penal charges.
  • Customer-support and grievance details.
  • Other material terms and conditions.

Customer instruction: Do not accept the loan until you understand the repayment obligations and the applicable charges.

STEP 9 — Provide Required Consent and Accept the Loan

If the customer decides to proceed, they must complete the applicable digital acceptance and consent process.

This may include acceptance of the loan agreement, KFS acknowledgement, applicable data-processing notices and any required repayment mandate.

Consent must be obtained through the appropriate process and recorded in accordance with applicable requirements.

A repayment mandate, such as eNACH, should be set up only through an authorised process and with the required customer authorisation.

STEP 10 — Loan Disbursal

After acceptance and completion of the required checks, the loan may be disbursed according to the approved lending arrangement.

The customer should be able to identify:

  • The lender or entity responsible for disbursal.
  • The approved loan principal.
  • The net amount disbursed.
  • The transaction reference, where available.
  • The applicable repayment due date.

Example: For a ₹1,000 principal under the specified calculation, the net disbursal is ₹742 after total deductions of ₹258.

The actual disbursal must follow the applicable agreement and regulatory requirements.

STEP 11 — View Loan Details

After disbursal, the customer should be able to access the relevant loan information through the available customer-facing facility.

This may include:

  • Loan account or proposal number.
  • Original principal.
  • Net disbursal.
  • Loan disbursal date.
  • Repayment due date.
  • Repayment amount.
  • Payment instructions.
  • Applicable loan documents.
  • Payment and outstanding status.

The website or application should display accurate and updated information.

STEP 12 — Repay the Loan

Customers should repay the amount specified in their loan agreement on or before the due date.

The payment page may provide the payment methods actually integrated and operational, such as:

  • Debit cards, where supported.
  • UPI, where supported.
  • Net banking, where supported.
  • Other expressly available payment methods.

Customers must use only the payment methods shown on the official payment page.

Example: A customer with a ₹1,000 scheduled repayment checks the due date and pays the amount through an available authorised payment method.

The actual amount due must be confirmed from the customer's loan account and loan documents.

STEP 13 — Payment Confirmation

After making a payment, the customer should retain the transaction reference or receipt.

The system should update the payment status after receiving the necessary confirmation from the payment provider or authorised reconciliation process.

If a payment is deducted from the customer's account but is not reflected in the loan account, the customer should contact support and provide the transaction details.

Customer Support: 9211331169 Email: support@dhannow.com

STEP 14 — Loan Closure

Once the full amount due has been received and reconciled, the loan account should be updated according to the applicable process.

Where applicable, the customer should be able to obtain confirmation of repayment and loan closure.

Customers should retain the repayment receipt and closure confirmation for their records.

STEP 15 — Eligibility for a Subsequent Loan

The specified loan progression is:

₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 → ₹7,000 → ₹10,000

A customer who repays the full amount on time or early may qualify for the next level, subject to the applicable eligibility and underwriting checks.

Example: A customer repays a ₹1,000 loan in full before the due date. The customer may qualify to apply for ₹2,000 after the required assessment.

A previous successful repayment does not guarantee approval of a subsequent loan.

3. Special Repayment Options

The specified business calculations also include extension and EMI-conversion structures. These options must be offered only where the applicable product and lending documents permit them.

A. Fourteen-Day Extension

The specified extension calculation uses a charge of ₹258 per ₹1,000 of principal for an additional 14-day period.

Example:

  • Original principal: ₹1,000.
  • Specified extension charge: ₹258.
  • Additional period: 14 days.

An extension must not be applied automatically. The customer must be informed of the applicable terms and accept the extension through the authorised process.

B. Twenty-Day EMI Conversion

The specified calculation uses simple interest of 1% per day on the original principal for 20 days, divided into two equal instalments.

Example for ₹1,000:

  • Principal: ₹1,000.
  • Interest for 20 days: ₹200.
  • Total calculated repayment: ₹1,200.
  • Two equal instalments: ₹600 each.

The customer must be shown the applicable dates, charges, APR and repayment schedule before accepting an EMI arrangement.

4. What If Your Application Is Rejected?

An application may be declined if the applicable eligibility or underwriting requirements are not met.

Where applicable, the customer may be informed of the decision through the available communication channel.

Customers should ensure that the information submitted is accurate and complete. Reapplying does not guarantee approval.

Example

A customer applies for a loan but does not satisfy the lender's applicable eligibility criteria. The application may be declined even if the customer has completed the online form.

5. What If You Face a Payment Problem?

If a payment fails, is delayed or is deducted without appearing in the loan account:

  • Keep the payment receipt or transaction reference.
  • Check the transaction status through the payment provider.
  • Contact DHAN NOW customer support.
  • Provide the relevant loan or proposal number and transaction details through an appropriate support channel.
  • Follow the published Payment, Refund & Transaction Dispute Policy.

Customer Support Phone / WhatsApp: 9211331169 Email: support@dhannow.com

Never share your OTP, PIN or password when raising a payment complaint.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How much can I borrow?

The specified product limits are ₹1,000 to ₹10,000, subject to the applicable loan level, eligibility and approval.

Q2. What is the loan tenure?

The specified standard tenure is 14 calendar days.

Q3. What is the interest rate?

The specified interest rate is 1% per day, calculated as simple interest on the original principal for the applicable period. The customer must also be shown the applicable APR.

Q4. Will every applicant receive a loan?

No. Approval depends on the applicable eligibility criteria, verification and underwriting decision.

Q5. When will I receive the money?

Disbursal takes place according to the applicable lending process after the required checks, documentation and acceptance are complete. A specific disbursal time should not be promised unless it is supported by actual operations.

Q6. How can I repay?

Use an authorised payment method displayed on the official payment page and follow the instructions in your loan documents.

Q7. What should I do if my payment is not updated?

Keep the transaction reference and contact customer support at 9211331169 or support@dhannow.com.

Q8. Can I receive a higher loan amount after repayment?

You may qualify for the next loan level after full repayment on time or early, subject to the applicable checks. An upgrade is not guaranteed.
