Frequently asked questions

This page is a draft and is being reviewed before publication. Questions? Call 92113 31169 or email support@dhannow.com.

1. ABOUT DHAN NOW

Q1. What is DHAN NOW?

DHAN NOW is a digital lending platform designed to provide customers with a convenient online loan application experience. Customers can apply online, complete the required verification process, review their loan offer, and access applicable loan-related information digitally.

Loan approval, disbursement, servicing, and other lending activities are subject to the applicable lending arrangement and the terms communicated to the customer.

Q2. Is DHAN NOW an online lending platform?

Yes. DHAN NOW provides a digital interface through which eligible customers can submit loan applications and complete the required application formalities online.

Q3. Who can apply for a loan through DHAN NOW?

Indian residents who meet the applicable age, identity verification, income, repayment capacity, credit assessment, and other eligibility requirements may apply, subject to the eligibility criteria displayed during the application process.

Submitting an application does not guarantee loan approval.

Q4. Is loan approval guaranteed?

No. Every application is subject to verification, underwriting, risk assessment, and the applicable lending criteria. The final decision depends on the customer's eligibility and the outcome of the assessment.

2. LOAN AMOUNT AND ELIGIBILITY

Q5. What is the minimum loan amount available?

The minimum loan amount under the stated product structure is ₹1,000, subject to eligibility and approval.

Q6. What is the maximum loan amount available?

The maximum loan amount under the stated product structure is ₹10,000, subject to eligibility, repayment history, and approval.

Q7. How much can I borrow on my first loan?

The first loan may be up to ₹1,000, subject to successful verification, underwriting, and approval.

Q8. Can I receive a higher loan amount after repaying my first loan?

Eligible customers may progress through the following loan limits:

  • ₹1,000
  • ₹2,000
  • ₹3,000
  • ₹4,000
  • ₹5,000
  • ₹7,000
  • ₹10,000

Progression requires full repayment on time or earlier, along with the applicable eligibility and underwriting checks. A higher limit is not automatic or guaranteed.

Q9. Can I skip a loan level?

Loan limits generally follow the stated progression. Any exception is subject to the applicable approval process and eligibility assessment.

Q10. Can I apply if I have an existing loan?

You may apply if the applicable eligibility criteria permit it. Existing loan obligations, repayment capacity, outstanding dues, and other risk factors may affect the assessment.

3. APPLICATION AND VERIFICATION

Q11. How do I apply for a loan through DHAN NOW?

The general application process is:

  • Visit www.dhannow.com or access the designated DHAN NOW application platform.
  • Enter the required mobile number and verify it using the prescribed method.
  • Provide the requested personal and financial information.
  • Complete the required KYC and other verification steps.
  • Submit the application for assessment.
  • Review the approved loan offer, applicable charges, repayment schedule, Key Fact Statement (KFS), and loan agreement.
  • Accept the offer and complete the required formalities.
  • Receive the disbursement if the application is approved and all applicable conditions are satisfied.

Q12. Which documents or information may be required?

Depending on the application and applicable requirements, customers may be asked to provide identity details, address information, bank account details, income-related information, and other documents or permissions necessary for verification and assessment.

The exact requirements will be communicated during the application process.

Q13. Why has my application been rejected?

An application may not be approved for reasons including incomplete information, unsuccessful verification, eligibility limitations, repayment-capacity concerns, credit assessment results, or other applicable risk criteria.

Approval decisions are subject to the applicable lending process. Contact customer support if you need assistance understanding the next steps.

Q14. Can I submit another application after rejection?

You may reapply when permitted by the applicable application rules. A new application will be assessed independently, and approval is not guaranteed.

Q15. How can I check my application status?

Use the available application or loan-status facility on the DHAN NOW platform. You may also contact customer support using your registered mobile number or application reference details.

4. LOAN TENURE AND REPAYMENT

Q16. What is the standard loan tenure?

The standard tenure under the stated product structure is 14 calendar days from the applicable disbursement date, unless a different tenure is specifically approved and communicated in the customer's loan documents.

Q17. When does my repayment become due?

Your repayment due date will be communicated in your loan documents, KFS, repayment schedule, or applicable digital loan records.

Customers should verify the exact due date and pay by the specified deadline.

Q18. How much do I need to repay on the due date?

Under the stated 14-day product structure, the scheduled repayment is the original principal amount. Applicable charges and interest are calculated and disclosed under the specified pricing structure.

Your actual approved loan documents and KFS will show the amounts applicable to your account.

Q19. Why is the amount credited to my bank account lower than the loan amount?

Under the stated product structure, applicable processing fees, GST on the processing fee, and 14-day interest are deducted upfront.

For example, for a loan with a principal amount of ₹1,000:

ParticularsAmount
Loan principal₹1,000
Processing fee (10%)₹100
GST on processing fee (18%)₹18
Interest for 14 days (1% per day)₹140
Total upfront deductions₹258
Net amount disbursed₹742
Scheduled repayment on Day 14₹1,000

The final amounts applicable to your loan will be displayed in your approved offer and KFS before acceptance.

Q20. Can I repay my loan early?

Early repayment may be made through the available repayment facility, subject to the applicable loan terms and payment instructions.

Please check your loan agreement and contact customer support if you need confirmation of the outstanding amount or the correct early-repayment procedure.

Q21. What happens if I repay my loan before the due date?

Early repayment can help you close your outstanding obligation sooner. Any effect on charges, interest, account closure, or eligibility for a subsequent loan will depend on the applicable loan terms and the repayment rules communicated to you.

Q22. What happens if I miss my repayment due date?

Missing the due date may result in an overdue account and may affect your future borrowing eligibility. Any applicable overdue charges, collection procedures, or credit bureau reporting will be governed by your loan agreement and applicable law.

Please contact customer support promptly if you anticipate difficulty making a payment.

Q23. Can I make a partial repayment?

Partial repayments may be accepted only through the available repayment process and in accordance with the applicable loan terms. A partial payment may not close your loan or satisfy the full repayment obligation.

For loan-limit progression, the stated rule requires full repayment on time or earlier; a partial repayment alone does not qualify.

Q24. Can I extend or roll over my loan?

An extension or rollover is not automatic. It may be available only if permitted under the applicable lending arrangement and approved through the authorised process.

Before accepting an extension, review the revised tenure, charges, interest, repayment date, and total amount payable.

Q25. Can I convert my loan into instalments?

An instalment or EMI conversion may be available only where specifically offered and approved. The applicable interest, number of instalments, due dates, and total repayment amount will be disclosed before you accept the arrangement.

Do not assume that an EMI conversion is available for every loan.

5. INTEREST, FEES, AND CHARGES

Q26. What is the processing fee?

Under the stated product structure, the processing fee is 10% of the loan principal. Applicable GST is calculated separately on the processing fee.

The applicable processing fee and tax amount will be disclosed in the loan offer and KFS.

Q27. How is the interest calculated?

Under the stated 14-day product structure, interest is calculated at 1% per day on the original principal for 14 calendar days, equivalent to 14% of the principal for the full 14-day period.

The applicable interest calculation and repayment terms will be shown in the customer's loan documents.

Q28. Is GST charged on the processing fee?

Under the stated product structure, GST is calculated at 18% of the processing fee. The applicable tax treatment will be reflected in the relevant loan disclosures and transaction records.

Q29. Are there any additional charges?

Any applicable charges must be disclosed in the relevant loan offer, KFS, loan agreement, or other required customer communication. Customers should review these documents carefully before accepting the loan.

Q30. Where can I see the total cost of my loan?

Review your approved loan offer, Key Fact Statement, and loan agreement. These documents should provide the applicable loan amount, net disbursement, fees, taxes, interest, APR where applicable, repayment dates, and total amount payable.

6. DISBURSEMENT AND BANK ACCOUNT

Q31. How will my loan amount be disbursed?

If approved, the loan amount will be disbursed through the authorised disbursement process to the eligible bank account specified and verified during the application process.

Q32. How long does disbursement take?

Disbursement timing may depend on successful verification, completion of required formalities, bank processing, and other applicable conditions. The estimated timeline, where available, will be communicated through the platform or customer support.

Q33. What should I do if the approved loan amount has not been credited?

Check your application status and bank account details. If the amount has not been credited within the communicated timeframe, contact customer support and provide your application or loan reference number.

Do not submit a duplicate payment or application solely because of a processing delay without first checking the status.

7. REPAYMENT METHODS AND PAYMENT ISSUES

Q34. How can I repay my loan?

Use the repayment facility made available through the authorised DHAN NOW payment page or the instructions communicated to you.

The currently stated payment options include:

  • UPI
  • Debit cards: MasterCard, Visa, RuPay, and Maestro, where supported
  • Net banking
  • Airtel Payments Bank, where available through the payment facility

Payment methods may be subject to availability and the applicable payment provider's terms.

Q35. What information do I need to make a payment?

You may be asked to enter the mobile number linked to your loan or provide your loan proposal number, as instructed on the payment page.

Always verify the loan reference and amount before completing the transaction.

Q36. What should I do if money has been debited but the payment is not showing as successful?

Keep the transaction reference number, payment receipt, date, time, and bank or payment-provider confirmation.

Contact customer support and raise a payment query. Avoid making a duplicate payment until you have checked the transaction status and received appropriate guidance.

Q37. What should I do if I paid the wrong amount or used the wrong loan reference?

Contact customer support promptly and provide the payment receipt, transaction ID, amount, date, and correct loan reference. The matter will be reviewed under the applicable payment-dispute and reconciliation process.

Q38. Will I receive confirmation after repayment?

A payment confirmation or receipt may be provided through the available payment facility or other authorised communication channel. Keep the confirmation for your records and check your loan account to ensure that the payment has been reflected.

Q39. How can I confirm that my loan is fully repaid?

Check the loan status through the available platform or contact customer support. Where applicable, request confirmation that all outstanding amounts have been cleared and that the account has been closed.

8. AUTO-DEBIT AND eNACH

Q40. What is an eNACH mandate?

An eNACH mandate is an electronic authorisation that may allow scheduled payments to be collected from a customer's bank account, subject to the mandate terms and the applicable banking process.

Q41. Is an eNACH mandate compulsory?

The mandate requirements, where applicable, will be communicated during the loan application or acceptance process. Customers should review the mandate terms before providing authorisation.

Q42. What should I do if an auto-debit fails?

Check your bank balance, account status, mandate status, and any bank notification. Contact customer support for the applicable repayment instructions.

A failed auto-debit does not automatically cancel your repayment obligation.

Q43. Can I cancel my eNACH mandate?

You may request cancellation through the applicable authorised process. However, cancellation of a mandate does not by itself cancel any outstanding loan repayment obligation.

Contact customer support for guidance on the process and any remaining dues.

12. IMPORTANT CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Q55. What should I check before accepting a loan?

Before accepting a loan, carefully review:

Q56. Can I accept a loan without reading the terms?

Q57. Does using DHAN NOW guarantee that I will receive a loan?

Q58. How can I protect myself from payment fraud?
