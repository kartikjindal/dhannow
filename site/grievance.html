DHAN NOW Customer Complaints, Escalation and Resolution Mechanism

Effective Date: [Insert Effective Date] Last Updated: [Insert Last Updated Date]

1. Introduction

DHAN NOW is committed to providing customers with a transparent, fair and accessible mechanism for raising complaints, reporting service issues and seeking resolution of concerns relating to the digital loan application process and associated services.

This Grievance Redressal Policy explains how customers can submit complaints, the channels available for contacting customer support, the escalation process and the expected timelines for handling grievances.

Complaints will be handled fairly, professionally and without discrimination. Customers will not be penalised merely for raising a genuine complaint or exercising their lawful rights.

2. Scope of the Policy

This policy applies to complaints and service-related concerns concerning DHAN NOW and the services provided through its website or digital platform, including:

  • Loan application and application status.
  • KYC and identity verification.
  • Loan eligibility and application processing.
  • Loan approval or rejection communications.
  • Loan amount and disbursement-related issues.
  • Processing fees, interest, GST and other disclosed charges.
  • Key Facts Statement (KFS) and loan documentation.
  • Repayment schedules and due dates.
  • Online payment failures and payment confirmation.
  • Repayment posting, receipts and outstanding balances.
  • Electronic repayment mandates, where applicable.
  • Loan account statements and closure-related requests.
  • Personal information and privacy concerns.
  • Customer communications and service quality.
  • Complaints concerning authorised service providers.
  • Other matters connected with the services offered through DHAN NOW.

Where a complaint concerns the actual lender, a regulated financial institution or another participating entity, it will be routed or escalated to the appropriate entity in accordance with the applicable arrangement and regulatory requirements.

4. Information to Include in a Complaint

To help facilitate a timely investigation, customers should provide the following information, where applicable:

  • Full name.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Loan application number, proposal number or loan account reference.
  • Date of the relevant event or transaction.
  • Description of the issue.
  • Transaction reference number or payment receipt, where relevant.
  • Copies of relevant communications or supporting documents.
  • The resolution requested by the customer.

Customers should not send passwords, PINs, OTPs, complete card security details or other authentication secrets by email or WhatsApp.

A complaint will not automatically be rejected merely because the customer cannot provide every item listed above. The team will request additional information only where reasonably necessary to investigate the matter.

5. Complaint Acknowledgement

Once a complaint is received through an available channel, DHAN NOW will make reasonable efforts to acknowledge it and provide a complaint reference number.

The acknowledgement should, where practicable, include:

  • The complaint reference number.
  • The date of receipt.
  • A brief description of the issue recorded.
  • The expected next step or applicable resolution timeline.
  • Contact details for follow-up or escalation.

Where the complaint has been received by the wrong entity, the customer should be informed of the appropriate contact or the complaint should be forwarded where the applicable process permits.

6. Complaint Resolution Process

Complaints will generally be handled through the following stages.

Stage 1: Initial Customer Support

The customer support team will review the complaint, verify the relevant application or transaction details and attempt to resolve the issue.

Where additional information is required, the team may contact the customer using the registered contact details or another permitted communication channel.

Stage 2: Investigation and Internal Review

If the complaint cannot be resolved at the initial stage, it may be referred to the relevant operational, payment, technology, compliance or loan-servicing team.

The investigation may include reviewing transaction records, payment gateway responses, application records, loan documents, customer communications and other relevant evidence.

Stage 3: Escalation to the Grievance Officer

If the customer is dissatisfied with the initial response or the matter requires further review, the customer may escalate the complaint to the Grievance Officer using the contact details provided in this policy.

The Grievance Officer will review the complaint, coordinate with the relevant team or entity and communicate the outcome or further steps to the customer.

Stage 4: Escalation to the Actual Lender, Where Applicable

Where the complaint relates to a loan issued by a regulated bank or non-banking financial company (NBFC), the complaint may also be escalated to the actual lender through its designated grievance redressal mechanism.

The lender's official grievance contact details and escalation procedure should be provided in the applicable loan documents or on the lender's official website.

The responsible entity must not avoid or transfer a complaint in a manner inconsistent with its legal or regulatory responsibilities.

8. Payment-Related Complaints

Customers may contact DHAN NOW if they experience issues such as:

  • Payment deducted but not reflected in the loan account.
  • Duplicate payment.
  • Incorrect payment amount.
  • Failed or pending payment.
  • Incorrect outstanding balance.
  • Repayment receipt not received.
  • Payment made against an incorrect loan reference.
  • Unauthorised or disputed payment.
  • Loan closure or outstanding balance not updated after repayment.

For faster investigation, customers should provide the transaction reference number, payment date, amount and the mobile number or loan reference associated with the payment.

Customers should not make a second payment solely because the first payment is not immediately reflected without first checking the transaction status and applicable payment instructions.

Any refund, reversal or payment adjustment will be handled in accordance with the applicable payment-system rules, transaction findings, loan documents and relevant law.

9. Loan Disbursement and Repayment Disputes

Where a customer disputes the loan amount credited, the deductions made before disbursement, the repayment amount or the outstanding balance, the complaint will be reviewed against the relevant loan documents and transaction records.

The review may include:

  • The sanctioned loan amount.
  • The applicable Key Facts Statement.
  • The loan agreement and repayment schedule.
  • The disclosed processing fee and applicable GST.
  • Interest and other applicable charges.
  • The net disbursal amount.
  • Payment and repayment records.
  • Any approved extension or revised repayment arrangement.

The customer will be informed of the outcome and any correction, adjustment or further action required.

10. Privacy and Data Protection Complaints

Customers may raise concerns about unauthorised disclosure, incorrect use of personal information, identity verification, access to personal data or other privacy-related matters.

Such complaints should be sent to:

Email: support@dhannow.com

Where the issue concerns a formal privacy request or a designated privacy contact, it should be forwarded to the appropriate responsible person or entity.

Privacy complaints will be handled in accordance with the applicable privacy notice and data protection requirements.

11. Unresolved Complaints and External Escalation

If a complaint relates to a regulated bank or NBFC and remains unresolved, the customer may use the grievance escalation channels of the actual regulated entity.

Where the matter falls within the jurisdiction of the Reserve Bank of India and the applicable eligibility conditions are satisfied, the customer may consider approaching the RBI through the applicable complaint mechanism, including the RBI Complaint Management System.

Official RBI Complaint Management System: https://cms.rbi.org.in

The availability of an external complaint mechanism depends on the nature of the complaint, the entity involved and the applicable scheme or regulatory framework.

Customers should first review the relevant lender's grievance policy and the conditions applicable to the external complaint mechanism.

12. Fair Treatment of Customers

DHAN NOW aims to ensure that complaints are handled:

  • Fairly and impartially.
  • Confidentially, subject to lawful disclosure requirements.
  • Without discrimination.
  • Without unnecessary delay.
  • With clear communication about the status and outcome.
  • By personnel with appropriate responsibility and authority.
  • With appropriate records of investigation and resolution.

Customers must not be subjected to intimidation, retaliation or inappropriate collection practices for raising a complaint in good faith.

13. Confidentiality and Complaint Records

Information received during complaint handling will be accessed only by authorised personnel and disclosed only where necessary for investigation, resolution, legal compliance or another lawful purpose.

Complaint records may include:

  • Customer and application references.
  • Complaint description and supporting documents.
  • Date of receipt and acknowledgement.
  • Investigation notes and actions taken.
  • Communications with the customer.
  • Escalation details.
  • Final response and closure status.

Records will be retained in accordance with applicable legal, regulatory and internal record-retention requirements.

14. Closure of Complaints

A complaint may be marked as resolved when the relevant investigation has been completed and a response has been communicated to the customer.

Where appropriate, the response will explain:

  • The findings of the investigation.
  • Any correction or action taken.
  • The reason a requested action cannot be completed, where applicable.
  • Any remaining steps the customer may take.
  • The available escalation channel, where the customer remains dissatisfied.

If the customer disagrees with the outcome, they may request further review through the designated escalation channel.

15. Responsibilities of the Grievance Officer

The Grievance Officer is responsible for coordinating the review and escalation of complaints received through the designated grievance channel.

Responsibilities may include:

  • Reviewing escalated complaints.
  • Coordinating with relevant internal teams.
  • Referring matters to the actual lender or other responsible entity where appropriate.
  • Monitoring complaint-resolution timelines.
  • Ensuring that customers receive clear responses.
  • Identifying recurring complaints and recommending corrective action.
  • Maintaining appropriate complaint records.
  • Supporting compliance with applicable grievance redressal requirements.
