1. Introduction

DHAN NOW is committed to providing customers with clear and transparent information about loan terms, interest, fees, taxes, repayment obligations, and the total cost of borrowing.

The Key Fact Statement (KFS) is a standardised document containing important information about a specific loan offer. It enables customers to understand the financial terms of a loan before accepting it.

Where applicable under RBI requirements, the KFS must be provided by the regulated lender before the execution of the loan contract, in the prescribed format and manner.

Customers should read the KFS carefully and compare its details with the loan offer, repayment schedule, and loan agreement before proceeding.

2. What Is a Key Fact Statement?

A KFS is a document that presents the principal terms and costs of a loan in a clear and standardised format.

Depending on the applicable regulatory requirements and loan product, the KFS may contain:

  • Name of the actual lender.
  • Borrower's details and loan reference.
  • Approved loan amount.
  • Loan tenure.
  • Interest rate and calculation method.
  • Processing fee and applicable taxes.
  • Net amount disbursed.
  • Annual Percentage Rate (APR).
  • Repayment schedule.
  • Instalment amounts and due dates.
  • Penal charges, where applicable.
  • Other applicable charges.
  • Cooling-off or look-up period information, where applicable.
  • Grievance redressal contact details.
  • Other information required by applicable law.

The actual contents will depend on the relevant product, the lender's obligations, and applicable regulatory requirements.

3. Who Provides the KFS?

The regulated lender responsible for the loan must provide the KFS in accordance with applicable requirements.

Where DHAN NOW provides a digital interface or other authorised services for a lender, it may facilitate the customer's access to the KFS through the relevant application or digital journey.

The actual lender must be identified clearly in the KFS and the applicable loan documentation.

4. When Should the Customer Receive the KFS?

The customer should receive the KFS at the stage required by applicable law and the relevant lending process, before entering into the loan contract.

The customer should have a reasonable opportunity to review the information and understand the loan's financial implications before accepting the offer.

Where applicable, the KFS must be provided in the prescribed format and remain accessible to the customer.

A customer should not be required to accept a loan without the disclosures and opportunity for review required under applicable law.

The KFS should clearly explain the approved loan amount and the amount expected to be credited to the borrower.

These amounts may differ where permitted charges or deductions are applied in accordance with the disclosed loan terms.

6. Interest Rate and Calculation Method

The KFS should identify the applicable interest rate and explain the method used to calculate interest.

For the proposed DHAN NOW 14-day product, the illustrative assumptions are:

  • Interest rate: 1% per day.
  • Standard tenure: 14 calendar days.
  • Illustrative interest for 14 days: 14% of the original principal, calculated using simple interest.
  • Illustrative processing fee: 10% of principal.
  • Illustrative GST: 18% of the processing fee, subject to applicable tax law.

These figures must not be presented as an unconditional offer to every applicant. The actual loan terms must be disclosed in the customer's KFS before acceptance.

The interest rate, calculation method, tenure, and applicable charges must be consistent with the actual lender's approved terms and regulatory obligations.

7. Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

The Annual Percentage Rate, or APR, is an important measure of the annualised cost of borrowing. It reflects the applicable interest and other charges included in the APR calculation under the prescribed methodology.

The APR is not necessarily equal to the stated nominal interest rate multiplied by a simple annual factor.

For short-term loans, the APR may be materially different from the nominal interest rate because of the short tenure, processing fees, taxes where included under the applicable methodology, and the timing of cash flows.

The actual lender must calculate and disclose APR in the manner required by applicable RBI directions and the prescribed KFS format.

Customers should rely on the APR stated in their KFS rather than estimating the annual cost from the daily interest rate alone.

Website implementation requirement: Do not publish a fixed APR for the DHAN NOW product until it has been calculated and verified using the actual loan cash flows and applicable regulatory methodology.

8. Processing Fees, Taxes, and Other Charges

The KFS should disclose applicable charges clearly, including the processing fee, taxes, and other permitted fees relevant to the loan.

For the proposed DHAN NOW structure, the illustrative assumptions are:

  • Processing fee: 10% of the principal.
  • GST: 18% of the processing fee, subject to applicable tax law.
  • Interest: 1% per day for the applicable period under the disclosed product terms.

Any additional fee, penal charge, or other amount must be properly disclosed and must be permitted under applicable law and the loan agreement.

Customers should review all applicable charges before accepting the loan.

No undisclosed charge should be imposed contrary to applicable law or the required loan disclosures.

9. Repayment Schedule

The KFS and accompanying loan documentation should clearly explain how and when the loan must be repaid.

Depending on the product accepted by the customer, the repayment terms may include:

A. Standard 14-Day Loan

Under the specified illustrative structure, the customer repays the original principal on the disclosed due date, with interest and the applicable processing fee and tax treated as stated in the KFS.

B. Approved Extension or Rollover

An extension may be available only where permitted by the lender's approved product terms and applicable law. Any additional interest, fee, tax, revised due date, or other cost must be disclosed before the customer accepts the extension.

An extension must not be treated as automatic.

C. Approved Instalment Conversion

If an instalment-based repayment option is offered, the KFS or associated repayment schedule must state the number of instalments, each instalment amount, due dates, applicable interest, fees, taxes, and total repayment obligation.

The exact option available to a customer depends on the offer made by the actual lender.

Customers should rely on their own KFS and repayment schedule rather than examples published on a general website.

10. Penal Charges and Late Payment

The KFS and other required loan disclosures should explain any applicable penal charges and the circumstances in which they may be imposed.

Such charges must comply with applicable law and RBI directions.

Customers should review the loan agreement and KFS for the applicable consequences of delayed repayment.

No late-payment charge or penal charge should be imposed unless it is permitted, properly disclosed, and applied in accordance with the applicable legal and contractual requirements.

11. Cooling-Off or Look-Up Period

Where required by applicable RBI directions, the customer must be informed of the applicable cooling-off or look-up period and the process for exiting the digital loan during that period.

The relevant lender must disclose the applicable period, the amount payable on exit, and any permitted retention of a reasonable one-time processing fee, where applicable under the governing requirements.

The existence, duration, and financial terms of this facility must be confirmed against the actual product and applicable regulatory rules.

Customers should refer to their KFS and loan agreement for the specific terms applicable to their loan.

12. Customer Acceptance and Consent

Customers should be able to review the KFS before accepting the loan.

Acceptance must take place through the authorised process and in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Where electronic acceptance is used, the relevant records should be maintained as required.

A customer should not be treated as having accepted a loan merely because the customer viewed a general product page or used a loan calculator.

Any consent required for electronic mandates, personal-data processing, or other separate purposes must be obtained in accordance with applicable law.

13. Accessing and Downloading the KFS

Customers should be able to access their KFS through the channel provided by the actual lender or the authorised digital lending interface.

Where technically supported, the KFS should be available for download or retention so the customer can review it later.

Customers are advised to retain copies of:

  • The KFS.
  • The loan agreement.
  • The repayment schedule.
  • Applicable consent and mandate records.
  • Loan disbursement confirmation.
  • Payment receipts and relevant transaction references.

Website implementation requirement: Provide a clear KFS access mechanism in the loan application journey and ensure that the document delivered to each customer matches the actual loan offer.
