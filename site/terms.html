6. LOAN AMOUNT AND TENURE

Under the stated DHAN NOW product structure:

  • Minimum loan amount: ₹1,000.
  • Maximum loan amount: ₹10,000.
  • First loan limit: Up to ₹1,000, subject to eligibility and approval.
  • Standard tenure: 14 calendar days.

The available amount and tenure for an individual customer will depend on the approved offer and applicable loan documents.

The loan progression structure is:

₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 → ₹7,000 → ₹10,000.

Progression to a higher amount is subject to full repayment on time or earlier, eligibility checks, and the applicable approval process. No customer is automatically entitled to a higher loan limit.

Where a different tenure or repayment arrangement is specifically offered and accepted, the terms stated in the relevant loan documents will apply.

7. INTEREST, PROCESSING FEE, GST, AND OTHER CHARGES

Under the stated standard 14-day product structure:

  • Processing fee: 10% of the loan principal.
  • GST: 18% of the processing fee.
  • Interest: 1% per day on the original principal for 14 days.
  • Total 14-day interest: 14% of the original principal.
  • Upfront deductions: Processing fee, applicable GST, and 14-day interest.
  • Scheduled repayment on Day 14: Original principal.
