  • Assistance with the application process.

B. Loan Disbursement

7. IMPORTANT INFORMATION FOR CUSTOMERS

  • Use the official DHAN NOW contact channels for loan-related assistance.
  • Keep your application reference, loan details, and payment receipts available when raising a query.
  • Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, password, or other confidential authentication credentials with anyone.
  • Always verify payment instructions before making a payment.
  • For loan-specific rights, repayment obligations, and lender-related complaints, refer to your loan agreement, KFS, and the applicable lender disclosures.
  • Submitting a complaint does not automatically suspend a contractual repayment obligation. If you are disputing a payment or amount due, contact customer support promptly for guidance.
