1. PURPOSE OF THIS POLICY

This policy explains the loan amounts, eligibility conditions, pricing structure, disbursement process, repayment requirements, and available repayment arrangements associated with the DHAN NOW digital lending service.

It is intended to help customers understand the financial terms of a loan before accepting an offer.

Customers must read this policy together with their specific loan offer, Key Fact Statement (KFS), loan agreement, repayment schedule, and other applicable disclosures.

Where the individual loan documents contain customer-specific terms, those documents must be reviewed alongside this general policy.

3. LOAN ELIGIBILITY

Customers must satisfy the applicable eligibility and verification requirements before a loan can be approved.

These requirements may include:

  • Meeting the minimum legal age for borrowing.
  • Providing valid identity and contact information.
  • Completing required KYC procedures.
  • Providing eligible bank account details.
  • Satisfying applicable income and repayment-capacity checks.
  • Passing the applicable underwriting and risk assessment.
  • Completing required authorisations and documentation.

Submission of an application does not guarantee approval.

The final loan amount and terms depend on the approved offer and applicable lending criteria.

4. LOAN AMOUNT PROGRESSION

The stated loan progression structure is:

₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 → ₹7,000 → ₹10,000

The first loan may be limited to ₹1,000.

A customer may become eligible for the next level after fully repaying the existing loan on time or earlier, subject to the applicable eligibility and underwriting checks.

The following rules apply:

  • Full repayment is required for progression under the stated structure.
  • A partial repayment alone does not qualify a customer for the next level.
  • A late repayment does not qualify as on-time repayment.
  • A higher limit is not automatic or guaranteed.
  • Any exception to the normal progression requires the applicable approval.

5. STANDARD 14-DAY LOAN PRICING

Under the stated standard loan structure, the charges are calculated as follows.

5.1 Processing Fee

The processing fee is 10% of the original loan principal.

Formula: Principal × 10%

5.2 GST

GST is calculated at 18% of the processing fee.

Formula: Processing Fee × 18%

5.3 Interest

Interest is calculated at 1% per day on the original principal for 14 calendar days.

Formula: Principal × 1% × 14 days

The resulting interest for the full 14-day period equals 14% of the original principal.

5.4 Total Upfront Deductions

Formula:

Processing Fee + GST on Processing Fee + 14-Day Interest

5.5 Net Disbursement

Formula:

Loan Principal − Total Upfront Deductions

5.6 Scheduled Repayment

Under the stated structure, the scheduled repayment on Day 14 is the original principal.

The actual due date, applicable charges, and payment requirements must be shown in the customer's loan documents.

7. LOAN APPLICATION AND APPROVAL

The general application process is:

  • Customer accesses the DHAN NOW platform.
  • Customer provides the requested application information.
  • Customer completes the required identity verification and KYC.
  • The application undergoes the applicable eligibility and risk assessment.
  • The approved offer, charges, repayment terms, and applicable disclosures are presented.
  • The customer reviews the KFS and loan agreement.
  • The customer completes the required acceptance and authorisation process.
  • The approved loan is disbursed through the authorised process, subject to applicable conditions.

Customers should not accept an offer until they understand the net amount to be received, the total cost, and the repayment obligations.

8. LOAN DISBURSEMENT

Approved funds are disbursed through the authorised process to the eligible bank account specified and verified during the application process.

The net amount credited may be lower than the principal where authorised charges are deducted upfront and disclosed in the loan documents.

Customers should verify the bank account details before completing the application.

If a disbursement is delayed or does not appear in the customer's account, the customer should contact support with the application reference and relevant bank information.

9. REPAYMENT DUE DATE

The standard tenure is 14 calendar days from the applicable disbursement date, unless a different arrangement is specifically approved and communicated in the loan documents.

The precise repayment due date must be displayed in the customer's loan records and repayment schedule.

Customers should check the date and amount payable rather than relying solely on a general website example.

10. REPAYMENT METHODS

Customers should use the authorised payment facility or the repayment instructions provided through the applicable loan channel.

The currently stated payment methods include:

  • UPI.
  • Supported debit cards: MasterCard, Visa, RuPay, and Maestro.
  • Net banking.
  • Airtel Payments Bank, where available through the payment facility.

Payment availability may depend on the payment provider and the transaction.

Customers may be asked to enter the mobile number linked to their loan or their loan proposal number.

Before completing a transaction, customers should verify the loan reference, amount, and payment details.

11. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION AND DISPUTES

Customers should retain the transaction ID, payment receipt, bank reference, and other confirmation details.

If an amount is debited but the payment status remains pending or unsuccessful, customers should contact support and request transaction verification.

A customer should not make a duplicate payment without checking the status of the original transaction.

Where an error or duplicate payment is confirmed, the issue should be handled through the applicable reconciliation and dispute-resolution process.

12. EARLY REPAYMENT

Early repayment may be made through the authorised process where permitted by the applicable loan terms.

Customers should confirm the amount required to close the loan and follow the prescribed payment instructions.

The treatment of interest, fees, and other amounts following early repayment will depend on the applicable contractual terms and relevant requirements.

Customers should retain proof of payment and confirm that the loan account has been updated appropriately.

An extension or rollover is not automatic.

  • GST: 18% of the processing fee.
  • Interest for the additional 14-day period: 14% of the applicable principal.

The calculated charges total ₹258 per ₹1,000 of principal.

Extending your loan

Pending Legal and tax sign-off. These details may change before they apply.

If you can’t repay on your due date, our collections team may offer to extend your loan by 14 days. You can’t extend a loan yourself in the app, and an extension is never automatic.

How it works

  • A collections officer speaks to you and offers the extension.
  • A Collection Head approves it.
  • You confirm it in the DhanNow app with an OTP.
  • You pay the extension charge in the app, or the officer records your payment.
  • Our accounts team checks the payment. Your new due date then applies.

What it costs

The extension charge is the same as the charges on a new 14-day loan: the processing fee (10% of the loan), GST on the fee (18% of the fee) and 14 days of interest (1% a day). That is ₹258 for every ₹1,000 you borrowed.

If your loan is already late, the late charge up to the day you pay is added: 2% of the loan amount for each day late, for at most 30 days. For example, on a ₹1,000 loan that is 3 days late you pay ₹258 + ₹60 = ₹318.

The extension charge does not reduce your loan. You still repay the full loan amount on the new due date.
