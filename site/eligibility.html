Check Your Eligibility for a DHAN NOW Personal Loan

  • Overview

DHAN NOW provides a digital application process for customers seeking short-term personal loans.

Applicants must meet the applicable eligibility criteria and complete the required identity verification, information checks and underwriting assessment.

Loan approval is based on the actual lender’s assessment. Meeting the basic eligibility conditions does not guarantee approval or disbursal.

  • Basic Loan Eligibility

Applicants may be required to meet the following conditions:

  • Age Requirement

The applicant must meet the minimum and maximum age requirements established by the applicable lender and product policy.

  • Identity Verification

The applicant must provide valid identification and complete the required KYC process.

  • Contact Information

The applicant must provide a valid mobile number and other contact information requested during the application.

  • Residential Information

The applicant must provide accurate residential address information and complete any required address verification.

  • Income and Repayment Capacity

The applicant may be required to provide income, employment or other financial information so the lender can assess repayment capacity.

  • Bank Account Verification

The applicant must meet the lender’s requirements for account verification and loan disbursal. Where required, the bank account must be held in the applicant’s own name.

  • Credit and Risk Assessment

The application may be assessed using credit information and other legally permitted risk-assessment methods.

  • Accurate Information

The applicant must provide genuine, complete and accurate information and documents.

  • Acceptance of Loan Terms

An approved applicant must review and accept the applicable loan offer, Key Facts Statement (KFS), loan agreement and required consents before proceeding.

Important: The precise age range, income threshold, employment criteria and other numerical eligibility conditions must follow the applicable lender-approved policy. No unconfirmed threshold should be displayed on the website.

  • Loan Amount Eligibility

The specified DHAN NOW loan limits are:

Particular| Amount

Minimum loan amount| ₹1,000

Maximum loan amount| ₹10,000

First-loan limit| Up to ₹1,000

Standard tenure| 14 calendar days

The amount available to a customer depends on eligibility, verification, underwriting and the applicable loan-level rules.

  • Loan Amount Progression

The specified progression is:

₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 → ₹7,000 → ₹10,000

A customer may qualify for the next level after fully repaying the current loan on time or before the due date, subject to the applicable checks.

Current Loan| Potential Next Level| Condition

₹1,000| ₹2,000| Full repayment on time or early, plus applicable checks

₹2,000| ₹3,000| Full repayment on time or early, plus applicable checks

₹3,000| ₹4,000| Full repayment on time or early, plus applicable checks

₹4,000| ₹5,000| Full repayment on time or early, plus applicable checks

₹5,000| ₹7,000| Full repayment on time or early, plus applicable checks

₹7,000| ₹10,000| Full repayment on time or early, plus applicable checks

₹10,000| No higher standard level| Maximum specified loan level

Example: A customer receives a ₹1,000 loan and repays the full amount on or before the due date. The customer may qualify for the next level of ₹2,000 after the applicable assessment.

Late repayment: A repayment made after the due date does not meet the on-time repayment condition for progression.

Partial repayment: A partial payment does not satisfy the full-repayment condition for progression.

A higher loan amount is not automatic or guaranteed.

  • Documents and Information Required

The applicant may be asked to provide information or documents relevant to the applicable verification process.

These may include:

Information Category| Examples

Identity information| PAN or other permitted identity information

Address information| Address details and permitted supporting documentation

Mobile verification| Registered mobile number and OTP verification

Bank account information| Account details and applicable verification

Employment information| Employment or occupation details, where required

Income information| Salary, income or other permitted financial information

Credit information| Credit information obtained through an authorised process, where applicable

Digital acceptance| Required loan documents and consent records

The actual requirements depend on the product, the lender’s approved process and applicable law.

Security notice: Customers should provide information only through authorised DHAN NOW channels. They should not share their OTP, PIN or banking password with an unauthorised person.

  • How Eligibility Is Assessed

The application may pass through several assessment stages.

Stage 1 — Application Review

The information submitted by the applicant is checked for completeness.

Stage 2 — Identity Verification

The required KYC and identity checks are performed.

Stage 3 — Information Verification

The lender may verify the relevant contact, address, bank account, employment or financial information.

Stage 4 — Repayment-Capacity Assessment

The application may be assessed to determine whether the proposed repayment is appropriate in light of the available financial information.

Stage 5 — Credit and Risk Assessment

The application may undergo applicable credit, fraud-prevention and risk checks.

Stage 6 — Lending Decision

The actual lender decides whether to approve the application and what terms, if any, to offer.

Passing an individual stage does not guarantee approval.

  • Example: First-Time Applicant

Applicant: Customer A

Requested loan: ₹1,000

Requested tenure: 14 calendar days

The customer submits the application and completes the required verification.

The lender assesses the information provided and determines whether the customer meets the applicable criteria.

If approved, the customer receives an offer showing the loan principal, applicable charges, net disbursal, APR and repayment obligation.

If the application is not approved, the customer is not entitled to receive the requested amount merely because the application was submitted.

  • Example: Customer Applying for a Higher Loan

Previous loan: ₹1,000

Requested next loan: ₹2,000

The customer has repaid the previous loan in full before the due date.

The customer may qualify for the next level, subject to the applicable eligibility and underwriting assessment.

The system should verify the previous loan’s repayment status and apply the relevant loan-progression rules before displaying an eligible next-level offer.

Important: A successful previous repayment does not guarantee approval of the next loan.

  • Example: Late Repayment

Previous loan: ₹1,000

Scheduled due date: 14 November

Actual repayment date: 16 November

In this illustrative example, the customer repays after the due date.

The repayment does not satisfy the on-time repayment condition for automatic progression. Any subsequent loan application remains subject to the applicable policy and underwriting assessment.

The website must not promise that a late repayment will automatically result in a loan upgrade.

  • Example: Partial Repayment

Previous loan: ₹1,000

Amount repaid: ₹600

Outstanding amount: ₹400

The customer has not repaid the full scheduled amount.

The full-repayment condition for progression has therefore not been met. The system should not treat this partial payment as qualifying the customer for the next loan level.

The outstanding amount and any applicable consequences must be determined according to the loan agreement and the customer’s account records.

  • Example: Application Not Approved

A customer requests a ₹5,000 loan but does not satisfy an applicable underwriting requirement.

The lender may decline the application or request additional information, as appropriate.

The customer should receive any decision-related information required under the applicable process.

The requested amount is not guaranteed, and the customer should not be encouraged to submit false information to obtain approval.

  • Loan Eligibility and Repayment Capacity

Customers should consider whether they can repay the loan on the scheduled due date.

Under the specified calculation structure, a ₹1,000 loan has the following illustrative figures:

Particular| Amount

Loan principal| ₹1,000

Processing fee| ₹100

GST on processing fee| ₹18

Interest for 14 days| ₹140

Total upfront deductions| ₹258

Net disbursal| ₹742

Scheduled Day-14 repayment| ₹1,000

This example shows why applicants should review both the amount they will receive and the amount they must repay.

The actual offer and KFS must clearly disclose the applicable APR, charges and repayment obligation.

  • Information Accuracy and Fraud Prevention

Applicants must provide genuine and accurate information.

The following may result in additional verification, rejection or other action permitted under the applicable terms and law:

- False or misleading information.

- Altered or fraudulent documents.

- Inconsistent identity details.

- Unauthorised use of another person’s information.

- Attempts to bypass verification controls.

Any action must follow the applicable lender policy, customer agreement and law.

  • Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is the minimum loan amount?

The specified minimum loan amount is ₹1,000.

Q2. What is the maximum loan amount?

The specified maximum loan amount is ₹10,000.

Q3. Can a first-time customer apply for ₹10,000?

The specified first-loan limit is up to ₹1,000. Higher amounts follow the defined progression and remain subject to the applicable eligibility and underwriting checks.

Q4. Is loan approval guaranteed if I meet the basic requirements?

No. The actual lender makes the final decision after the applicable checks.

Q5. Can I qualify for a higher loan after repayment?

You may qualify for the next level after full repayment on time or early, subject to the applicable assessment.

Q6. Does partial repayment qualify me for the next level?

No. The specified progression rule requires full repayment of the current loan.

Q7. What happens if I repay late?

A late repayment does not satisfy the on-time repayment condition for progression. Any other consequences depend on the applicable agreement and policy.

Q8. Which documents are required?

The documents depend on the applicable KYC, verification and underwriting requirements.

Q9. Can I apply if my previous application was declined?

You may be able to submit a subsequent application if permitted by the applicable process, but approval is not guaranteed.
