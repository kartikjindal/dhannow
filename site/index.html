How we keep you safe

Lending is built on trust, so here’s exactly what we do and don’t do.

  • Only you can borrow in your nameWe check your Aadhaar through DigiLocker, your PAN, and match a selfie to your Aadhaar photo.
  • Money goes only to your accountWe send ₹1 first and confirm the account holder’s name matches yours.
  • You read before you signThe Key Facts Statement shows every charge and the APR. Copies stay in your app.
  • Two people check every loanThe person who approves a loan can’t release the money, and every action is logged.
  • Your documents are encryptedDownload links expire after 15 minutes. Your consent is recorded with the date and time.
  • A named person handles complaintsOur Grievance Officer’s name, phone and email are on this page, not hidden in a policy.

How we handle complaints

Questions

Why do I get less than I borrow?

The fee, GST and 14 days of interest are taken out before the money is sent. On ₹1,000 that’s ₹258, so ₹742 reaches you and you repay ₹1,000 on day 14.

Do I pay anything before getting the loan?

No. There’s never an advance fee. Everything is taken out of the approved loan and shown in your Key Facts Statement first.

Can I log in on the website?

Not yet. The website is still being built, so please use the Android app.

Will repaying early lower my interest?

Interest for all 14 days is taken upfront. You only get a refund for early repayment if your loan agreement says so.

Before you borrow

Check the total you’ll repay, the APR, the amount that reaches your bank and the due date. Borrow only if you’re sure you can repay on time, and call us if anything is unclear.
