1. INTRODUCTION

DHAN NOW respects the privacy of individuals who visit its website, apply for financial products, or use its services.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you access www.dhannow.com or use the associated digital services.

It also explains how you may contact us regarding your personal information and privacy-related concerns.

Please read this Privacy Policy together with the Terms & Conditions, Customer Consent Information, Loan Information & Repayment Policy, and other applicable disclosures.

Where a separate regulated lender processes your information, that lender's applicable privacy notices and legal responsibilities will also apply.

2. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed in connection with:

  • Visits to the DHAN NOW website.
  • Online loan applications.
  • Customer identity and eligibility verification.
  • Loan assessment and processing.
  • Loan disbursement and servicing.
  • Repayment and payment reconciliation.
  • Customer support and grievance handling.
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Communications relating to DHAN NOW services.

This policy should be read alongside any specific privacy notice or consent screen presented during the application process.

3. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on the services you use and the permissions you provide, DHAN NOW or the relevant authorised entity may process the following categories of information.

3.1 Personal and Identification Information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Date of birth.
  • Residential address.
  • Mobile number.
  • Email address.
  • PAN or other permitted identity information.
  • KYC and identity-verification results.
  • Other information required for lawful identity verification.

Only information necessary for the relevant and disclosed purpose should be collected.

3.2 Contact Information

This may include your mobile number, email address, and communication preferences so that relevant application updates, service notifications, repayment reminders, and responses to enquiries can be provided.

3.3 Financial and Employment Information

Where necessary for the loan application and permitted by law, information may include:

  • Income and employment details.
  • Occupation or business information.
  • Declared financial obligations.
  • Bank account details.
  • Financial documents supplied by you.
  • Relevant financial verification results.
  • Credit information obtained through authorised and legally permitted channels.

Access to financial information must be limited to the purposes disclosed to you and supported by the required legal basis or authorisation.

3.4 Bank and Payment Information

Depending on the transaction, information may include:

  • Bank account details necessary for verification or disbursement.
  • Payment transaction references.
  • Repayment records.
  • Payment status and reconciliation information.
  • Electronic mandate status.
  • Relevant payment confirmation records.

Complete card credentials, passwords, PINs, and authentication secrets should not be collected or stored by DHAN NOW unless specifically permitted and appropriately secured under the applicable payment and legal requirements. Customers should never send these details through ordinary email or customer support messages.

3.5 Device and Technical Information

When you use the website or digital application, limited technical information may be processed, where necessary and permitted, including:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Browser type and version.
  • IP address.
  • Website interaction and diagnostic logs.
  • Session information.
  • Security and fraud-prevention signals.

The collection and use of device-related information must be described in the applicable notice and should be proportionate to the purpose.

3.6 Application and Loan Information

This may include:

  • Application reference number.
  • Application status.
  • Verification status.
  • Approved loan offer.
  • Loan documents and customer acknowledgements.
  • Repayment schedule.
  • Outstanding balance and repayment records.
  • Customer service requests and complaint history.

3.7 Information You Provide Voluntarily

We may process information that you provide through contact forms, email, WhatsApp, customer support, or grievance channels.

Please avoid including unnecessary personal or confidential information in general enquiries.

4. HOW WE COLLECT INFORMATION

Information may be collected through the following methods:

  • Directly from you when you complete an application or submit a form.
  • During identity verification and KYC procedures.
  • Through documents and information you voluntarily provide.
  • Through authorised verification providers.
  • Through banks, payment service providers, or other authorised service providers where permitted.
  • Through credit information channels where the required legal basis and authorisation exist.
  • Through website technologies and security logs, where applicable.
  • Through customer support communications and grievance submissions.

Where information is obtained from another source, the relevant process must comply with applicable law and any required customer notice or consent.

5. PURPOSES FOR WHICH INFORMATION IS USED

Personal information may be processed for the following purposes, subject to the applicable legal basis and disclosures.

5.1 Loan Application Processing

To receive, process, assess, and manage your application.

5.2 Identity Verification and KYC

To verify identity, prevent impersonation, complete applicable verification requirements, and support lawful customer onboarding.

5.3 Eligibility and Credit Assessment

To assess eligibility, repayment capacity, and relevant risk factors through authorised processes.

5.4 Loan Disbursement and Servicing

To facilitate approved disbursement, maintain loan-related records, and support loan servicing.

5.5 Repayment and Payment Reconciliation

To process repayments, reconcile transactions, update payment records, and respond to payment-related queries.

5.6 Fraud Prevention and Security

To identify suspicious activity, protect accounts and systems, and investigate potential misuse or fraud.

5.7 Customer Support

To answer enquiries, resolve technical problems, address disputes, and manage complaints.

5.8 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To fulfil applicable legal, regulatory, recordkeeping, audit, reporting, and lawful information-request requirements.

5.9 Service Communications

To send relevant application updates, transactional notifications, repayment information, security alerts, and service-related messages.

Marketing communications, where applicable, will be handled in accordance with the relevant consent, preference, and legal requirements.

6. CONSENT AND CUSTOMER AUTHORISATION

Certain processing activities may require your consent or specific authorisation.

Where consent is required, the relevant purpose and material information should be presented before consent is obtained.

You should review the relevant consent notice before authorising access to personal, financial, identity, or other information.

Consent for one purpose should not automatically be treated as consent for unrelated purposes.

Where applicable, you may withdraw consent through the prescribed process. Withdrawal does not retrospectively invalidate processing lawfully completed before withdrawal, and it does not remove information that must continue to be retained under applicable law.

Withdrawal may affect the availability of a service where the relevant information is necessary to provide it lawfully.

7. DIGITAL LENDING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICE PROVIDERS

To provide the relevant services, authorised entities may process information for purposes such as identity verification, application assessment, payment processing, technology operations, and customer support.

Depending on the actual lending arrangement, these entities may include:

  • The actual regulated lender identified in your loan documents.
  • Authorised KYC and verification providers.
  • Payment gateways and payment service providers.
  • Banks involved in authorised transactions.
  • Technology and infrastructure providers.
  • Communication service providers.
  • Other authorised parties involved in delivering the disclosed services.

Each party's role, access, and responsibilities should be determined by the actual contractual arrangement and applicable law.

Information should be shared only where necessary, authorised, and supported by an appropriate legal basis.

8. CREDIT INFORMATION AND CREDIT BUREAU DATA

Where permitted and subject to the required authorisation, information may be obtained from or reported to authorised credit information companies or other lawful credit information sources.

Credit-related information may be used for eligibility assessment, underwriting, loan servicing, fraud prevention, or other disclosed and legally permitted purposes.

The identity of the responsible lender and the applicable credit-reporting arrangements should be reflected in the relevant loan documents and customer notices.

Customers who believe that credit information has been reported incorrectly may contact the relevant lender or use the applicable dispute-resolution process.

9. BANK ACCOUNT, FINANCIAL DATA, AND MANDATES

Where bank account verification, financial information access, or electronic repayment mandates are required, the relevant process and purpose should be explained to the customer.

Customers should review the permissions and authorisations requested before proceeding.

Any access to financial information must be limited to the applicable purpose, legal basis, and authorised scope.

Cancelling an electronic payment mandate does not, by itself, extinguish an outstanding contractual repayment obligation.

10. COOKIES AND WEBSITE TECHNOLOGIES

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for necessary website functions, security, preferences, analytics, or other disclosed purposes.

Where required, appropriate notice and consent controls should be provided.

For more information, customers may review the DHAN NOW Cookie Policy.

11. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

Personal information may be disclosed to authorised parties where necessary for the purposes described in this policy and permitted by law.

Possible categories include:

  • The actual lender and authorised lending-service participants.
  • KYC and identity-verification providers.
  • Payment processors and relevant banks.
  • Technology and hosting providers.
  • Customer communication and support providers.
  • Professional advisers and auditors, where appropriate.
  • Courts, regulators, law-enforcement authorities, or other public authorities where legally required or permitted.

We do not authorise third parties to use personal information for unrelated purposes merely because they receive it for a service.

Any sharing must comply with applicable contractual, security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.

12. DATA SECURITY

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be implemented to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the nature of the information and the relevant system, safeguards may include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions.
  • Encryption where appropriate.
  • Secure authentication.
  • System monitoring and security logging.
  • Staff confidentiality requirements.
  • Vendor security controls.
  • Backup and recovery arrangements.
  • Incident management procedures.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, reasonable safeguards should be maintained and reviewed in accordance with applicable requirements.

If a personal data breach occurs, the responsible entity should follow the applicable incident-response and notification requirements.

13. DATA RETENTION

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the disclosed purposes, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, audit, and recordkeeping obligations.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of record and the purpose for which it is held.

Where information is no longer required and no lawful retention obligation applies, it should be securely deleted, anonymised, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with the applicable retention policy.

The website operator should maintain an internal retention schedule identifying the relevant record categories, responsible entities, and applicable retention periods.

14. CROSS-BORDER PROCESSING

Where personal information is stored or processed outside India, the responsible entity must comply with the applicable legal requirements and any restrictions governing such transfers.
