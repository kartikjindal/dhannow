C12d Extension: every state the app can show

The app never guesses. Every screen reads the extension status from the server on open, on app resume, on pull-to-refresh and when a push arrives; the notification is only a doorbell. So whatever staff did, the app shows the same thing the console shows, within one refresh.

S1 · Offer waiting (dashboard banner)

Head approved; borrower opens the app, with or without having seen the notification.

Extension offered by our teamPay ₹318 by 11:59 pm today to move your due date to 2 Nov.Review and confirm
Loan card (overdue) stays below. The banner comes from GET /loans/{id} → pendingExtension, not from the notification.

Server: status APPROVED, pay_by ≥ today

S2 · Offer expired

Opened after 23:59 IST on the pay-by day (old notification, or app left open overnight).

This offer has expiredIt ended at 11:59 pm on 22 Oct. Nothing changed on your loan. Call us for a new offer.Call 9211331169Back to dashboard

Server: status EXPIRED (00:05 sweep) or APPROVED with pay_by < today (treated as expired on read)

S3 · Already extended

Opened from an old notification after the extension went through, or on a second phone.

Already extended to 2 NovExtension 1 of 3 was verified on 22 Oct. You don’t need to do anything.Go to dashboard

Server: status ACTIVE; screen chosen by status, never by the notification text

S4 · Offer withdrawn

Officer cancelled, Head rejected, or the loan became ineligible (e.g. repaid, settlement opened).

This offer was withdrawnYour due date is still 19 Oct. If you need more time, call our collections team.Call 9211331169

Server: status CANCELLED / REJECTED; borrower never sees internal reasons

S5 · OTP problems

Code not received, wrong code 5 times, or SMS delayed.

Too many wrong codesTry again after 3:45 pm. Your offer stays open until 11:59 pm today.Resend code
Resend after 30 s, max 5 per 15 min (existing OTP service). Code also readable by SMS autofill. If the pay-by day ends while locked, S2 applies.

Server: OTP purpose LOAN_EXTENSION; accept is idempotent (a retry after a crash returns the same result)

S6 · Paid, being checked

Borrower submitted UTR in the app (or the officer logged it).

Payment received, checking with the bank₹318 · UTR 4290…8876. Your due date stays 19 Oct until it’s verified, usually the same day. No late charge is added for days while we check if your payment was on time.

Server: status PAYMENT_PENDING; on-time judged by bank paid_on, not verification time

S7 · Payment not matched

Accountant rejected the extension payment (wrong amount, unmatched UTR, wrong account).

We couldn’t match your paymentReason: reference not found in our bank. Check the UTR and send it again before 11:59 pm.Correct the referenceCall us

Server: payment REJECTED with picklist reason; extension back to ACCEPTED until pay_by

S8 · Offline or slow

No network, or the server didn’t answer.

You’re offlineShowing details from 10:42 am. Confirm and pay need a connection; nothing was sent.Try again

Server: last good response cached with its timestamp; all actions disabled

S9 · Something we don’t recognise

Server added a state this app version doesn’t know, or the response is malformed.

Your loan was updatedPull down to refresh. If this stays, update the app or call us.RefreshUpdate app
Unknown enum values decode to UNKNOWN (never a crash); minVersion from /app/config can force an update.
