A first-time user, screen by screen

Their question at each moment, and how the app answers it. Every screen has one main button that says what happens next, a back that never re-sends, and a way to reach a person.

MomentWhat they’re thinkingWhat the app does
Open the app“Is this legit? What is it?”Brand, one line, then a 3-slide intro they can skip. No login wall.
Welcome“How much do I actually get and pay? What do I need?”Worked example (₹1,000 → ₹742 today → ₹1,000 on day 14), a “keep ready” list, a safety page and a help number before signing up.
Sign-up steps 1–3“Can I stop halfway? Will I lose this?”Step x of 12 with a progress bar. Before an account exists, Exit returns to Welcome. After that, Save & exit opens the in-progress dashboard, and the app reopens at the same step (Resume).
OTP“The code didn’t come.”Resend counter, then a clear lock screen with when to retry and a support line, not a silent failure.
Steps 4–11“Why do they need this? What if upload fails?”Upload errors say what to fix (size, type, password). Camera blocked explains how to allow it.
Submitted“Now what? How long?”Review time, optional extras to speed it up, Track my application → status timeline → dashboard.
Waiting“Did anything happen?”The in-progress dashboard shows one status chip and one next step; the bell carries updates. Declined, security question and approved each have their own screen.
Offer steps“What exactly am I signing?”Amount ladder, date, KFS before signing, selfie with 3 guided moves, agreement, payout account. Back on a finished step shows a confirm sheet, never a re-run.
Loan active“How much, by when, how do I pay?”Dashboard shows the amount, due date and one Repay button. Everything else sits behind View more.
Repay“Did my payment go through?”Pending verification state, rejection screen with the reason, then a closure receipt and the next level.
Late“Am I in trouble? Can I get time?”Calm overdue card, extension and repayment-plan screens when offered, Talk to us.
Never a dead endEvery error has Try again (back to where it failed) and Contact support.
Never the wrong homeBefore a loan, the dashboard shows the application, not a loan card.
Never a double submitButtons lock while sending; finished steps reopen view-only.
Always a personSupport works logged out, from Welcome and every error screen.
