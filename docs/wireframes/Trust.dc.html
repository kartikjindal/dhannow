How we keep you safe
Only you can log in

One-time codes expire in 5 minutes and work once. Repeated wrong tries are blocked. Fingerprint unlock on this phone.

Identity checked with official sources

Aadhaar through DigiLocker, PAN validation, and a selfie matched to your Aadhaar photo, so no one can borrow in your name.

Money goes only to your verified account

We send ₹1 first and check the account holder’s name matches yours.

You sign with Aadhaar e-Sign

Every cost is shown in the Key Fact Statement before you sign. Copies stay in the app.

Approval and payout are done by different people

The person who approves a loan can’t also release the money, and every action is logged with who and when.

Your documents are stored encrypted

Download links expire after 15 minutes. Your consent is recorded with date and time.

We never ask for your OTP or PIN

We don’t read your contacts, messages or gallery. Report suspicious calls to support.
