DhanNow UI kit

Every component the DhanNow Android app repeats across its borrower boards, in one place. It is the app_navix kit from app.dhanboost.com (the same Panel, Figure, SegmentBars, PillNav and friends behind /sample), switched to the cobalt accent and extended with the pieces a phone loan journey needs. Every control here works. The Flutter widget name and the boards that use each piece are on its card.
