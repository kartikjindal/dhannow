Set up auto-repayment

To receive your advance, authorise an eMandate with your bank so your repayment can be collected automatically on the due date. The mandate allows debits up to a maximum amount (ceiling) that is higher than your advance.

Opens your bank’s page in a secure tab. Shown only when the auto-repayment flag is on.
